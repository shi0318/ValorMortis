Valor Mortis is a soulslike, so boss fights are the heart of it. We do **not** yet publish a tested, boss-by-boss breakdown of the Demo build — that requires hands-on verification we haven't been able to confirm. Instead, this page gives you a **transferable approach** built from the confirmed combat systems and how soulslike bosses generally behave. Every tactical claim here is inference, tagged **Community**, not a first-party fact.

> Before any fight that looks like a boss arena, use the Demo's **Save Anytime** feature. It's listed as a Demo comfort feature and it's your best safety net.

## Read the fight before you fight it

Soulslike bosses telegraph. Your first attempt is reconnaissance:

- **Watch, don't trade.** Spend attempt one learning the boss's move set and the gap after each attack.
- **Identify the parry-able attacks.** Since parrying is a confirmed core mechanic, most bosses will have attacks built to be parried. Find them.
- **Note the unblockables.** The supernatural dodge exists for the attacks you can't parry — figure out which is which early.

## Turn the confirmed kit into a game plan

Working from the confirmed **Rapier + pistol fusion**, **parry**, **dodge**, and **alchemical powers**:

- **Parry to open, Rapier to punish.** The blade is fast — a clean parry should net you a quick punish string, not one greedy heavy swing.
- **Use the pistol to interrupt.** A shot timed into a boss's wind-up can buy space or stagger. Don't waste it as chip damage.
- **Save powers for the punish window.** Alchemical abilities are almost certainly resource-gated, so spend them when they're guaranteed to land.
- **Respect resurrection, don't rely on it.** Death being a "tool" doesn't mean dying is free — treat it as a system to learn, not a strategy to lean on.

## Positioning and stamina

- **Keep the camera on the boss** in a first-person soulslike — losing the tell is how you die.
- **Don't empty your stamina.** Leave enough for a dodge after every commitment.
- **Fight in open space** where you can dodge in any direction.

## What we can't yet confirm

Boss names, health, phase transitions, specific attack timings, and how many bosses the Demo actually contains all require tested verification. As corroborated details surface from players or official sources, they'll be added and tagged accordingly — **Demo-tested** for hands-on confirmation, **Community** for cross-checked player reports.
