One More Level's official Steam post, **"3 things we are working on to improve combat in Valor Mortis,"** reports the three most common concerns raised during the playtest. This is a developer update about planned improvements, not a claim that every change is already present in the currently available Demo.

## 1. Combat readability: clearer windups and defensive cues

The developer says players found combat, especially defensive actions and cues, difficult to read. Its stated response is:

- improve attack windups;
- add stronger pre-attack audio cues;
- improve the visual and audio distinction between **blocks** and **parries**.

Those are concrete intended changes, but the post does not publish parry-frame values, enemy-by-enemy timings, or a date for a particular Demo build. Do not turn this announcement into a timing chart until the changed build can be checked directly.

## 2. Controls: default layout and rebinding

Keyboard players told the team that blocking with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and using <kbd>Alt</kbd> felt uncomfortable. One More Level says it will revisit the default layout, highlight control rebinding, and ensure custom bindings save between sessions. The post also suggests side mouse buttons as a possible ergonomic option for blocking or dodging.

The side-button suggestion is developer advice, not a universal best bind. The important confirmed point is that the studio identified rebinding persistence as a target. Check the in-game menu in the build you are playing before assuming a particular key can be reassigned.

## 3. Target lock and camera behaviour

The third recurring concern was target lock during multi-enemy encounters and the boss fight. The announced work includes smoother camera tracking, smarter target prioritisation, and extended target-lock range for melee and ranged combat.

Again, this is forward-looking language from the developer update. It does not confirm that all target choices, range values, or camera behaviour are final in the Demo or at launch.

## How to give a useful report

The free Demo is the correct place to check current behaviour. When a control, target-lock, or defensive-cue issue occurs, record:

1. the Demo build and platform;
2. keyboard/controller and the relevant binding;
3. the enemy or encounter type;
4. whether the issue involved a block, parry, target switch, or camera movement;
5. a short capture if the official feedback channel accepts one.

This is a reporting checklist from this guide, not an official support form. For confirmed current PC support, use [Demo settings and known issues](/demo/settings-and-known-issues/); for patch-specific performance work, use [Demo performance and upscaling](/demo/performance-and-upscaling/).
