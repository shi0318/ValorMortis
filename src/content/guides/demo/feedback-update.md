One More Level published **“Responding to the biggest feedback from the Demo”** on **August 9, 2026**. This page separates what players liked, what the team says it plans to improve, and what has **not** been confirmed as already fixed. It was checked on **August 14, 2026**.

## What players responded positively to

The developer's summary says players responded well to the **first-person combat**, the feel of a **perfect parry**, challenging boss fights, and the combination of FPS presentation with Soulslike combat. The post also calls out the Napoleonic setting and atmosphere as parts of the Demo's identity.

These are themes from the developer's feedback summary, not a review score or a promise that every player will agree with the assessment.

## Changes the team says it is working on

The official post identifies several improvement areas:

- Combat readability, so attacks and responses are easier to understand.
- Hitbox range and enemy staggering.
- More boss variety.
- CPU optimisation and the performance impact of enemies.
- The balance between platforming and combat.
- The difficulty curve created when the Demo jumps from Chapter I to Chapter VII.

The Chapter I-to-VII jump matters for Demo walkthrough searches: it can make the available slice feel harsher than a continuous opening route would. The developer presents that as feedback and a problem to address, not as evidence that the full campaign has the same difficulty curve.

## Planned improvement is not the same as a patch

The announcement describes work the team is preparing. It does not say that a new Demo build has already shipped with every item above fixed. Until a new version is announced and checked, keep these claims in the **planned improvements** category.

For the current playable route, use the [Demo walkthrough](/demo/walkthrough/) and [Demo boss guide](/demo/boss-guide/). For hardware and graphics, use the [Demo PC requirements](/demo-pc-requirements/) page rather than assuming CPU optimisation has already changed the listed requirements.
