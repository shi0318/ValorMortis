## The setting you drop into

Per the official description, you play **a fallen soldier of Napoleon's Grande Armée**, resurrected into a **Paris twisted by dark magic**. The City of Light has become a nightmarish labyrinth: the dead walk, monsters prowl, and the streets run with blood and rain. The Demo is your first taste of that world — expect the opening stretch of this interconnected, 19th-century Paris.

## The core loop

Valor Mortis is described as a **first-person action soulslike from the creators of Ghostrunner**. Translating that into what you'll be doing moment to moment:

- **Read the enemy, then commit.** Soulslike combat rewards patience — watch the tell, parry or dodge, then punish the opening.
- **Fuse melee and gunplay.** You carry a **Rapier and a pistol**. The official text frames combat as blending **visceral melee with gunplay**, so weaving a shot into a melee string is central rather than optional.
- **Use death as a tool.** The signature hook is **resurrection as a tactical advantage** — dying is woven into the systems, not just a fail state. Exactly how this plays in the Demo build is something the Demo is there to show you first-hand.

## Suggested approach for the Demo

This section is **practical inference** from the soulslike genre and the confirmed mechanics above — not a step list from a tested build.

1. **Spend your first encounters learning the parry window.** Soulslikes live and die on defensive timing; the Demo is a low-stakes place to build that muscle memory.
2. **Don't hoard your pistol.** Since combat is designed around fusing shots into melee, treat the pistol as a rhythm tool (stagger, interrupt, finish) rather than an emergency button.
3. **Explore for shortcuts.** The full game is an **interconnected metroidvania**; even a Demo slice usually loops back on itself. Poke at side paths.
4. **Lean on Save Anytime.** Use it before anything that looks like a boss arena.
