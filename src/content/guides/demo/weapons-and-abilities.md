Everything on this page is built from the **official Steam store description** for Valor Mortis. The confirmed arsenal is compact and clearly defined; where we add advice on how to use it, that advice is labelled as inference from the soulslike genre — not from a tested build.

## The confirmed arsenal

Valor Mortis fuses **visceral melee with gunplay**. Two weapons anchor that fusion:

- **The Rapier** — your primary melee weapon. A thrusting blade built for speed and precision, fitting the 19th-century French setting and the fast, reactive combat the developers are known for.
- **The pistol** — a single-shot firearm woven directly into melee. The design intent, per the description, is to **blend** shooting into the melee flow rather than switch between two separate modes.

Defensive tools, also confirmed:

- **Parries** — deflect incoming attacks with precise timing to open enemies up.
- **Dodges** — evasive movement described with **supernatural speed**, reflecting the resurrected protagonist's unnatural agility.

## Dark alchemical powers

The description confirms **supernatural, alchemical powers** granted by the same dark magic that resurrected you. These are framed as combat augmentations — think spectral energy woven into your offense. The Steam media shows blasts of spectral energy, but the **full power list, costs, and unlock order are not something we can state as fact yet**, so we don't.

## Resurrection as a mechanic

The signature system is **resurrection used as a tactical tool**: death is folded into the combat design rather than being purely a punishment. How it resolves in play — what you keep, what it costs, how it changes an encounter — is exactly what the Demo is there to demonstrate first-hand.

## How to use the kit (inference)

This section is **practical inference** from the confirmed mechanics and the soulslike genre, not tested data:

- **Treat the pistol as a tempo tool.** In fusion combat, a well-timed shot to stagger or interrupt is usually worth more than raw damage.
- **Parry to create windows, dodge to survive.** Parrying is the aggressive defensive option; the supernatural dodge is your bail-out when a parry isn't safe.
- **Expect powers to be resource-gated.** Alchemical abilities in this genre almost always draw on a meter or charge — spend the Demo learning how yours refills.
