The full game is scheduled for **October 13, 2026** on Steam, while the free Demo was released as Steam App **4641840** on June 7, 2026.

## What the Demo is useful for

The Demo provides the only playable pre-release evidence for Valor Mortis. Its Steam listing describes a free product with Save Anytime and a separate App ID. The Demo and the paid full game are different Steam products, so a player should keep their expectations and installed-product data separate.

The Demo can answer practical questions about controls, movement feel, accessibility options, early encounters, and PC performance on the tested build. It cannot prove that every weapon value, enemy placement, checkpoint, or ability rule will remain unchanged in the retail release.

## Is Demo progress confirmed to carry over?

No official source tracked by this site promises that Demo saves, inventory, upgrades, or story progress transfer to the full game. The safe answer is therefore **not confirmed**. Do not buy or download the game on the assumption that a Demo save will become a retail save.

What can carry over is knowledge: the Demo walkthrough, Chapter 7 notes, performance observations, and control preferences can become launch-day test cases. They still need to be rechecked against the retail build.

## What changed around the Demo window?

One More Level released the Demo on June 7 and later brought it back for a summer window reported as July 20 through August 7. The Demo’s availability window and the full game’s October 13 date are separate facts. If the Demo is not playable on the current Steam page, use the archived walkthrough as pre-release documentation rather than linking readers to an unavailable installer.

## Full-release checklist

On October 13, record the retail build before comparing it with the Demo:

1. Verify whether the opening route and Chapter 7 content still use the same names.
2. Re-test resurrection, parry timing, target lock, and mobility inputs.
3. Compare the retail PC requirements with the Demo requirements instead of treating them as identical.
4. Re-check the Rapier, Musket, Shield Transmutation, and Nephtoglobin powers for final costs and unlock rules.
5. Mark every Demo observation that changed rather than quietly replacing the old evidence.

The official developer material describes a first-person soulslike with parkour, firearms, resurrection, and a Napoleon-era setting distorted by dark magic. Those are the right launch topics; exact damage, cooldown, and route claims require retail testing.
