Steam App 2828710 lists **39 achievements** for **Valor Mortis**. That count belongs to the retail app. The Demo is App 4641840. A Demo clear does not fill a retail row.

The retail checklist, boss names, and weapon feats are on the [achievement guide](/guides/achievements-and-trophies/). This page is only the storefront ceiling, checked October 16, 2026, and the rule for when to chase it.

## When to start the list

Install the **First Update** first. A launch-day blocker can look like a missable achievement. It is often a trigger that the update already names.

Catalyst routes and skill picks change how fast you reach later fights. They do not change the count. See [Catalyst farming](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/) and [best skills](/guides/best-skills-guide/).

Steam does not assign PlayStation-style bronze, silver, gold, or platinum tiers to these 39.
