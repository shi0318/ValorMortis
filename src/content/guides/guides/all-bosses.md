Valor Mortis runs **eleven chapters plus the Entropy finale**, with **ten boss fights**. Chapters 2, 8 and 11 have no boss of their own; the Chapter 2 fight is a Lothaire rematch that you skip if you beat him in Chapter 1. There is **no difficulty selector**, so the only adjustable things are your build, your flask charges and the Lantern you rested at. This page is the order, the chapter, and what each fight is actually testing.

The ladder tells its own story. Roughly a quarter of owners have beaten the Chapter 4 Sphinx, one in six the Slaughtermill, and only **4.1%** have beaten Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris in Chapter 10. Chapters 4 and 5 are where most runs stop.

## What each fight tests

**Chapters 2–3 — Lothaire and the Iron Marshall.** These are parry checks with a firearm opening. Both are winnable with the starting kit, which is exactly why they are the wrong place to be short on Endurance: every melee swing, dodge and block spends from the same bar.

**Chapter 4 — the Sphinx.** The first real difficulty wall, and the first boss where a pure damage build fails. If you are stuck here, the problem is usually that you spent early skill points on damage instead of Health and Endurance.

**Chapter 5 — the Slaughtermill.** Large, with a readable pattern and very punishing contact. Stasis buys the window to confirm a weak point.

**Chapter 6 — Anna, the Bride Drowned in Tears.** Pattern recognition more than resource management. Arrive with full flasks.

**Chapter 7 — Justicar Aurelius and Experiment 83.** Two named fights in one chapter, which is why the drop-off from 11.9% to 10.4% is so small — almost everybody who beats the first beats the second.

**Chapter 9 — the Orphan.** The point where firearms stop being a backup and start being how you create openings.

**The endgame — Captain Raphael and the Entity.** The Entity brings back attacks from the bosses before it. By here, one fully upgraded melee weapon, one fully upgraded firearm and one fully upgraded Transmutation beat three half-built ones. See the [upgrade rules](/guides/weapons-and-upgrades/).

## Before any boss door

1. **Rest at the nearest Lantern** and bank your Catalysts. Dying with unspent Catalysts is the expensive mistake.
2. **Refill Ferro Flask charges.** There is no difficulty slider to fall back on.
3. **Bring the right Transmutation**, not your favourite one — Fire for grouped adds, Stasis for a single heavy target.
4. **Do not arrive mid-upgrade.** A half-upgraded weapon plus a half-upgraded power is worse than either finished.
