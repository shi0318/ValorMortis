Valor Mortis gives William a skill board with survival, endurance, Nephtoglobin, firearm, and damage upgrades. The best opening is not a pure damage rush: these choices determine how many parries, dodges, shots, and Transmutations you can fit into one encounter. This guide ranks the current retail options and then turns them into three simple build paths.

## Best first upgrades

| Priority | Skill | Why it comes first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Maximum Health** | Gives every build more room for a missed parry, a bad hitbox read, or a failed platforming jump. Take the first available health increase before a pure damage node. |
| 2 | **Maximum Endurance** | Melee attacks, dodges, and blocks all consume Endurance. A larger bar lets you attack and still keep one defensive option available. Rapier and Claymore users feel this immediately. |
| 3 | **Maximum Nephtoglobin** | Raises the resource ceiling for Transmutations. More capacity means more Fire or Stasis casts before you must search for remnants or force melee extraction. |
| 4 | **Nephtoglobin Sufferance** | Improves the power-focused loop and pairs with a larger Nephtoglobin pool. It is the best bridge between a normal melee build and a Transmutation build. |
| 5 | **Ammo Restock** | Adds ammunition capacity for firearm-focused builds and reduces how often you need to return to a Lantern. |
| 6 | **Transmutation Damage** | Supports a power-focused route when Fire or Stasis is doing much of your encounter work. |

## Three reliable opening builds

### Rapier and parry build

Take **Maximum Health** first, then **Maximum Endurance**, then another Endurance investment before chasing melee damage. The Rapier rewards repeated parries and quick follow-ups, but a missed timing costs Endurance twice: once to defend and again to escape. Keep a firearm available for weak-point pressure rather than spending every point on a narrow headshot bonus.

Suggested order:

1. Maximum Health.
2. Maximum Endurance.
3. Ammo Restock.
4. A second Endurance or Health node.
5. Nephtoglobin Sufferance for emergency Fire or Stasis.

This route also makes **Symphony of Steel** easier because the larger resource bars give you more time to reset after a parry chain. The [100% achievement guide](/guides/achievements-and-trophies/) has the full combat checklist.

### Fire and Transmutation build

Open with **Maximum Nephtoglobin**, then **Nephtoglobin Sufferance**, and add **Transmutation Damage** once the resource pool is comfortable. Fire clears grouped civilians and soldiers quickly; Stasis gives you a safe window to confirm a weak point or a finisher. Add Maximum Health before the late-game damage nodes so a missed cast does not end the run.

Suggested order:

1. Maximum Health.
2. Maximum Nephtoglobin.
3. Nephtoglobin Sufferance.
4. Transmutation Damage.
5. Ammo Restock for firearm finishers.

The [Catalyst farming routes](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/) are useful here because the Marketplace and Remnant loops let you buy the resource nodes without delaying story progression.

### Firearm and weak-point build

Take **Ammo Restock** after a small Health investment, then raise Endurance so aiming does not leave you unable to dodge. The Pistol handles airborne-aim challenges, the Blunderbuss deletes a weak point at close range, and the Musket gives a safer opening against targets that punish melee. Use one firearm until **Gun of Glory** is complete instead of upgrading every gun at once.

Suggested order:

1. Maximum Health.
2. Ammo Restock.
3. Maximum Endurance.
4. Maximum Nephtoglobin.
5. Transmutation Damage.

This is also the cleanest path for **God’s Trigger**, **Windhover**, and **Scattershot**. Set up each feat beside a Lantern so a missed chain does not turn into a long run back.

## Nodes to delay

The following nodes are situational in the current retail balance: Nephtoglobin Extraction, Nephtoglobin Remnants, Ammunition Drop Chance, Blocking, Melee Endurance Cost, Healing Effectiveness, Maximum Health & Melee Damage, Headshot Damage, Airborne Aiming, Ferrofluid Flask Refill, Physical Resistance, Endurance Regeneration, and Nephtoglobin Sufferance & Transmutation Damage. They can support a finished build, but they give less early value than the six priorities above.

**Melee Damage, Status Buildup, Critical Damage, and Maximum Firearm Damage** are especially poor first purchases in the current build because their effect is weak or inconsistent compared with the visible stat bars. Save them for a later experiment after Health, Endurance, resource capacity, and ammunition are covered; a balance patch can change this ranking.

## Upgrade rules that prevent wasted Catalysts

- Buy the first Health and Endurance nodes before a pure damage node. They improve every weapon and every boss attempt.
- Pick one weapon family for maximum level first. A single fully upgraded melee weapon, firearm, and Transmutation are enough for the three upgrade achievements.
- Keep one firearm slot available even for a melee build. Risen Grenadier buckets, airborne pistol kills, and weak-point windows are much easier when ammunition is not an afterthought.
- Re-evaluate after each chapter. New Ambers and mobility routes can turn a previously niche node into a useful one, while the early-game damage options remain expensive for little gain.
