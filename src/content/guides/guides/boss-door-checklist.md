A boss door is not a farm and not a respec screen. Pick the opener before you spend the last Catalysts: rapier, firearm, or Transmutation. Endurance before greed. The ranked paths are on [best skills](/guides/best-skills-guide/).

If the kit feels thin, the loop is the lantern behind you, not a longer walk. [Lantern, not the door](/guides/lantern-loop-not-a-boss/).

## Before you pull the boss

- One opener only. A third element you have not cast is not a plan.
- Ammo or the Transmutation resource full enough for the recovery windows, not for a panic mag.
- The First Update installed. A launch-day trigger can look like a missing phase.
- Achievement names for boss fights stay on the [39-achievement guide](/guides/achievements-and-trophies/). This page does not add phases the retail notes have not named.

Steam does not give these fights PlayStation tiers. Clear the door, then check the pop.
