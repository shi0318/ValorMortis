Catalysts pay for skill points and the upgrades that keep a weapon or Transmutation relevant. The fastest route changes as you unlock chapters, so do not spend half a run crossing the map for a larger payout. Farm beside a Lantern, spend the Catalysts safely, and move to the next loop when the enemy no longer gives enough for the time.

## The two Ambers that make farming worthwhile

The **Surplus Amber** improves Catalyst gains from kills. Its exact acquisition route can depend on optional exploration and unlocked paths, so use this guide to compare the named Lantern loops after finding the Amber rather than assuming it is required for the early route.

The route comparisons below are qualitative. Enemy health, your weapon upgrades, time spent walking, and missed attacks all affect the return; use a consistent five-reset timing if you want to compare your own build.

## Best routes at a glance

| Unlock point | Lantern and target | Loop | Approximate return |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter 1 | Onslaught Arch / Trenches | Run to the Risen Soldiers, clear them, return to the nearby Lantern, and turn around. | Earliest repeatable route; shorter travel matters more than raw enemy value. |
| Chapter 3 | Fallen Village Marketplace | Pull the Shambling Civilians from the barn, use an area Transmutation, return across the short path, and repeat. | A compact group-clearing route once Fire is available. |
| Chapter 5 | Vale of Wrath — Under the Bridge | Climb beside the Lantern, kill the Remnant of the Iron Regiment, rest, and repeat. | A strong single-target reset once weak-point attacks are consistent. |
| Chapter 10 | Fortress — Chapel | Use the Remnant reset beside the Chapel Lantern; compare its approach with Under the Bridge. | A late-game option when its shorter approach offsets the tougher target. |

## Chapter 1: the earliest safe loop

At **Onslaught Arch**, leave the first Lantern and follow the battlefield’s main route. Veer right toward the damaged crate with a fire on one side and a torn banner on the other. Continue through it until a second crate and three Risen Soldiers come into view. Cast Fire to catch the group, then finish survivors with the Saber rather than spending firearm ammunition.

Use the Lantern just behind that encounter. Turn around, clear the same three soldiers, and interact with the Lantern to reset them. This route is available before expensive skills and weapon upgrades matter. It can also drop early melee materials, which is useful when the first weapon is still at low level.

## Chapter 3: Marketplace barn

The **Fallen Village Marketplace** has a barn directly across from its Lantern. Six **Shambling Civilians** are packed inside. Step through the entrance to pull them out, back up through the doorway, and hold Fire as they funnel toward you. If one reaches you during the cast, dodge while keeping the attack held; taking a hit makes the loop slower and can consume the flask charges you wanted to save.

This loop is quicker than Chapter 1 for many builds and is a good place to buy the first few health, endurance, or Transmutation upgrades. Stop once you have the next upgrade rather than staying here after Chapter 5 opens.

## Chapter 5: Under the Bridge Remnant

The best mid-game reset is the **Remnant of the Iron Regiment** beside the **Under the Bridge** Lantern in the Vale of Wrath. Climb the ladder next to the Lantern and target the Remnant’s weak points with the Blunderbuss. Two accurate blasts expose the front deathblow; finish with a melee weapon, return to the Lantern, rest, and climb again.

The rhythm is fast because there is one target and little travel. The same enemy also counts toward **Statue of Insanity** if you lock it fully in Stasis, so the route can combine a resource session with that achievement attempt. If the weak-point shots are unreliable, practice first so a failed run costs only time.

## Chapter 10: Chapel endgame loop

In Chapter 10’s **Fortress**, the **Chapel** Lantern places another Remnant directly in the route. Use the same Blunderbuss weak-point sequence, claim the deathblow, rest, and repeat. The shorter walk makes this the most efficient late-game farm for skill points and final weapon or Transmutation upgrades.

Do not quote a fixed number for every build: damage upgrades, Amber ranks, and missed shots change the result. The useful test is Catalysts per minute over five complete resets. If Chapter 10 is less efficient for your current weapon than Under the Bridge, use Chapter 5 until the next upgrade increases your kill speed.

## Spend the haul without losing it

Bank Catalysts at the Lantern before trying an achievement setup or a new weapon. A death before the recovery run puts the farm back to zero, while the point of these loops is predictable, low-travel income. Prioritise **Maximum Health**, **Maximum Endurance**, **Nephtoglobin** capacity or Sufferance, and **Ammo Restock** before niche damage nodes; the [best skills guide](/guides/best-skills-guide/) explains the order and the current retail trade-offs.
