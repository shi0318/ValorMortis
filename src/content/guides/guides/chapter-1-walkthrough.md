**Chapter 1: Revival** takes place on **the Battlefield**. It is the shortest chapter in Valor Mortis and the one that teaches everything else: parry, the pistol shot after it, burning gold vines with Fire, and the Lantern reset. It has **three Lanterns**, **three Amber chests**, and it ends with a fight against **General Lothaire** that you are allowed to lose.

## Chapter 1 at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Region | The Battlefield |
| Lanterns | Onslaught Arch → Trenches → Field Encampment |
| New weapon | Saber (you start with it) |
| New Transmutation | Fire |
| Ambers | Bottomless Well, Transmuted Persistence, Iron Grip |
| Boss | General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer (first meeting) |
| Missable items | None. Every area can be revisited |

## Route: start to Onslaught Arch

You wake with the **Saber** and nothing else. Follow the only path forward and swing across the branches at the broken bridge. The first Lantern, **Onslaught Arch**, is on the far side. Rest there. It sets your respawn point, and the enemies you killed on the way come back, which is the whole loop of the game in one step.

Lantern names do not show in the travel menu yet. Fast travel opens later in the campaign, after the Lothaire fight in Chapter 2. Until then, walking is the only way back.

## Fire Transmutation

Go **uphill from Onslaught Arch**, crouch under the fallen log, and slip past the axe-carrying Sapeur. The corpse on the high ledge gives you the **Fire** Transmutation. Walk back down and burn the woven barrier next to the Lantern; that is the way onto the battlefield proper.

Fire is the key for this whole chapter. Gold vines block every optional chest here, and only Fire clears them.

## The three Amber chests

1. **Bottomless Well**: from Onslaught Arch, turn around and climb back up the cliff using the branches. Burn the gold vines on the left; the chest is behind them. This Amber slowly restores Nephtoglobin.
2. **Transmuted Persistence**: once you are on the battlefield, keep to the **left side**. Just before the metal carriage you walk through, gold vines on the left hide a small group of enemies and a chest. Clear the group first.
3. **Iron Grip**: after the Plunge Attack tutorial near **Trenches**, turn **right** instead of climbing the wooden ledge. The chest sits in a dead end with one guard.

## Battlefield to Field Encampment

The middle of the chapter is a crossing through cage passages under musket fire.

- **Use cover against Musketeers.** Their shot has a long wind-up; close the gap during the reload, not before.
- **Sapeurs carry explosive barrels.** Shoot or burn the barrel from range and let it do the work.
- **At the tunnel where the gate shuts behind you**, kill all three Soldiers before you work the gate. Turning your back on them mid-animation is the most common death in the chapter.

**Trenches** is the second Lantern and the best place in the chapter to bank Catalysts. The three Risen Soldiers near it are also the earliest repeatable [Catalyst loop](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/). **Field Encampment** is the last Lantern before the boss.

## Boss: General Lothaire, the Eagle-Bearer

The chapter ends with the first meeting with **General Lothaire**. Most players lose here, and **losing is a valid route**: you wake in Chapter 2 and fight him again at the Quarry with more tools.

If you want to try for the win:

- **Parry the delayed sword swings.** He holds the wind-up long enough to bait an early press. Wait for the swing.
- **Dodge the cannon volley sideways**, not backwards.
- **Shoot the red growths** on his body. They are firearm weak points.
- **Dodge the red-glowing attacks; do not parry them.** They are unblockable and appear once he mutates.

Beating him here pays out Catalysts on the spot, and you skip the rematch in Chapter 2. Either way, the trophy for the fight, **Torn Banner**, unlocks the first time you actually win.

## What carries into Chapter 2

You leave Chapter 1 with the Saber, Fire, up to three Ambers and whatever Catalysts you banked. The next chapter adds the Pistol in its first few minutes. Continue with the [Chapter 2 walkthrough](/guides/chapter-2-walkthrough/), or see [every chapter in order](/guides/walkthrough/).
