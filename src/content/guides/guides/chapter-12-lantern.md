The October 16 update resolved a progression blocker on Chapter 12 and fixed edge cases where cutscenes might not trigger or might not unlock the next lantern. The habit is the same as Chapter 4: watch the scene, then confirm the lantern exists before you quit. Chapter 12 is not Chapter 4. Do not reload the wrong door.

On **October 25, 2026** the US Steam page listed Valor Mortis at **$39.99**, Very Positive, **1,089** English reviews. This check did not show a 30-day split, so none is quoted. Twelve days after the October 13 launch, that English line is a fast Very Positive start. It does not unlock a lantern the cutscene failed to flag on an unpatched install.

## The check

Update to the October 16 build or newer. Play the Chapter 12 scene through. Do not alt-tab the kill if you can help it. Move until you can see the next lantern, or until the game has clearly placed you past the door. Quit only after that proof. A quit during the cutscene is the old failure mode.

If you already lost the flag on a pre-patch kill, the notes do not promise a retroactive save. Redo the scene on the update. It is shorter than arguing with a dead flag. Do not delete `Saved` to force it. That folder is the chapter. Delete `GameUserSettings.ini` only if a demo left stutters, and only that file, after you update the driver the popup names.

## Not the other bugs

Chapter 4 is the post-boss save after its own cutscene. Chapter 5 is In the Name of Art failing to start. Volunteers who stopped attacking after a patrol backstab were a free kill the patch removed. If you are on Chapter 12, you are not fixing those by standing in the catacombs. Read the chapter number. Write it down if you are tired. A wrong-chapter reload makes a fixed bug feel new.

William is still one soldier. The rapier still makes the window. The lantern check does not ask you to max the pistol first. Materials stay finite. A Very Positive page does not duplicate them.

## After the lantern is there

Parry one enemy on the far side so you know the save was a save and not a menu ghost. If the street hitches, the driver popup is the next menu, not another chapter select. Minimum spec is a GTX 1660 Ti or RX 5600 XT, **16 GB** of RAM, and **60 GB** on an SSD. A hitch is not a missing lantern.

**$39.99** on this check is the sticker. **1,089** Very Positive English reviews are the crowd. **39** achievements will not pop because you quit early. Confirm the light. Then leave the desk. The scene you watched is the one the patch taught the game to keep.
