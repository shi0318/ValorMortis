**Chapter 2: Regroup** sends you through the **Valley of Ashes**, a long climb with **six Lanterns**. It is where William gets his first firearm, the **Pistol**, and it ends at the **Quarry** with a rematch against **General Lothaire**. If you beat him in Chapter 1, there is no rematch and the route runs straight on to the Abandoned Church.

## Guidepost and the Pistol

You wake at the **Guidepost** Lantern, where Lucjan finds you after the battlefield. Follow the road to **Demolished Path**. The **Pistol** is in the carriage there, and it changes the combat from this point on: parry, then shoot in the enemy's recovery. Do not open a fight with the gun. The reload lands inside the next swing. [Parry before the shot](/guides/parry-before-the-shot/) covers the timing.

## The Climb

**The Climb** is the vertical stretch of the valley. Follow the main path past the large wagon tipped on its side. After the first zipline at the top, go **left instead of uphill**, break the boxes on the platform edge, and follow the path to the tower with an enemy on it. The chest there holds the **first Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**. Take it now; more healing per flask matters more than anything else in this chapter.

## The Other Side

**The Other Side** has a poisoned passage that belongs to **The Emissary** investigation. You cannot clear it on this visit. The ability you need comes much later in the campaign. Mark it and move on.

## Military Camp: get Dominatus

The **Military Camp** is the most important stop before the boss. The **Dominatus** Amber is here, and it heals you on critical attacks. Lothaire is built around posture breaks, so Dominatus turns every successful parry chain into healing. Equip it before you leave the camp.

This is also where you can agree to help the Adjudant Sous-Officier, which starts **An Unheard Farewell**.

## Quarry and the Lothaire rematch

Rest at the **Quarry** Lantern before the fog wall. If you lost the Chapter 1 fight, Lothaire waits beyond it.

**Phase 1**
- Delayed two- and three-hit sword strings. Parry them to break posture, then take the critical hit.
- Cannon volleys from range: dodge **sideways**.
- A jumping slam: dodge as he comes down, not when he leaves the ground.
- The **red Nephtoglobin growths** on his body are Pistol targets.

**Phase 2** (after the first health bar)
- He mutates and adds **red-glowing unblockable attacks**. Dodge them; do not try to parry.
- Glowing orbs send a ground shockwave (jump it) or chasing spikes (keep moving).
- He summons soldiers. Melee soldiers block your movement; Musketeers fire once and vanish.

The starting **Saber** is enough. Save your flask charges for phase two, and keep Fire for safe openings rather than opening with it. The win unlocks **Torn Banner** and opens the road to the **Abandoned Church**.

## After the boss

At the Abandoned Church you meet **James**, and weapon upgrades become available. From here you can also return to the Battlefield with the Pistol to finish **The Oathbound**: an Eternal Guardian behind a weak-point wall near Onslaught Arch drops two key items.

Fast travel between Lanterns is open from this point, so backtracking for chests is cheap.
