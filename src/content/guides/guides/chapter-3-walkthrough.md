**Chapter 3: Rendezvous** is set in the **Fallen Village**. James sends you to find his comrade **Louis**, who is trapped somewhere past the farms. On the way you pick up two weapons, the **Rapier** and the **Blunderbuss**, use two keys, can finish two side quests, and fight the **Iron Marshall**. Beating him gives you the **Grapple**.

## Leaving the church: the Rapier

Leave the **Abandoned Church** through the door next to James and turn **right**. Walk through the cemetery to the end of the path. A corpse impaled on a blade blocks the way: pull the weapon out to take the **Rapier**. The other path from the church leads to the Fortress, which you cannot enter yet.

## Fields: The Scarecrow

The **Fields** Lantern is where Lucjan waits. **The Scarecrow** quest starts in the field to the right. Interact with the body on the scarecrow post a few times.

1. Head toward the barn and take the **right path to the big tree**. Pick up the **Lover's Pendant** from the body at its base.
2. At the split with a wooden gate and a Grenadier on the right, go the other way toward the large house. A chest by the **well** holds a **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**, and the path opens a shortcut.
3. Past the tall haystacks and the group of four villagers, go uphill to the left into a house. The **"Letter with a Drawing"** is on the top-floor table.
4. Return both items to the scarecrow.
5. In the next field, a barn full of exploding bodies hides the **Volatile End** Amber at the back right. Climb to the top of the barn and use the two pillows to finish the quest. The reward is **Bountiful Harvest**.

## The Blunderbuss

The road is blocked by a **Volunteer**, a large enemy with a huge sword. Kill him and take the **Blunderbuss** from the table in the carriage behind. The Blunderbuss is a required pickup: it is how you blast weak points for the rest of the game.

## Marketplace: Lullaby and the Storage Key

At the **Marketplace** Lantern:

1. Go toward the water with fires in the distance and enter the house. Walk up to the **empty crib** to start **Lullaby**.
2. Leave by the back door. The **Weathered Key** is on the ground near a group of enemies.
3. Go back past the Volunteer's spot, turn right to the end of the path, and **jump the river** to a house. Unlock it and take the **Toy** from the bed.
4. Put the Toy in the crib. The reward is **Tomorrow's Promise**.

Then, in the open village with patrols:

- First path on the left: a corner house holds another **Ferrofluid Flask Power Upgrade**.
- The house the enemy group is shooting at: go to the top-floor balcony and jump across for **Warrior's Fury**.
- Out the back, down the ladder, underground: the **Storage Key** is on a box near two enemies. It opens the nearby door, and behind it is the weak point that clears the **plague wall** blocking the main road.

The Marketplace barn opposite the Lantern also has the best Chapter 3 [Catalyst loop](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/): six Shambling Civilians and one Fire cast.

## Boss: the Iron Marshall

The **Iron Marshall** waits at the **Orchard** farmhouse. This is the first boss where you have melee and ranged options at the same time: Saber, Rapier, Pistol and Blunderbuss.

- His swings are **slow and well telegraphed**. Keep Endurance in reserve for the dodge, not for extra hits.
- He is **weak to ranged damage and Fire**. Shoot whenever melee range is unsafe.
- Rest at **Marketplace** or **Orchard** before the door, and bank your Catalysts first.

The win unlocks the trophy **Ultima Ratio Regum** and the **Grapple**.

## After the boss: Louis and the Grapple

Use the Grapple to reach the high path past the arena and find **Louis**. Then return to James at the Abandoned Church. Louis upgrades Transmutations from now on, and James hands you the **Cave Grotto Key**, which starts Chapter 4: Dark Descent.

Two more pickups need the Grapple:

- **Glass Soldier** (Orchard): climb into the building to the right of the entry, break the boxes and drop out of the back to a chest.
- **Empty Ferrofluid Flask** (near Marketplace): grapple across the water. After the second pair of plague pillars with grapple points, the chest is on the right.

The Grapple also opens the upper Quarry platforms back in Chapter 2.
