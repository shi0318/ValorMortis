The **October 16, 2026** update added GPU driver detection. An automated popup tells you the driver is old. The same note says outdated drivers were one of the places they saw the biggest stability gains. Update that driver before you delete folders, before you refund a Mostly Positive game, and before you decide the parry is broken.

Valor Mortis launched **October 13, 2026**, at **$39.99**. On **October 20** reviews were Mostly Positive, **1,602** of **2,054**. Three days of patches will not fix a year-old driver. The popup is the part the studio asked you to read. The ini delete is the part they asked demo and playtest players to do. They are not the same step.

## The order

1. Install the October 16 update or newer. The popup does not exist on the launch-hour build.
2. If the popup names a driver, update that GPU driver. Restart. Launch again. Play one street.
3. If you played the playtest or the demo and the street still stutters, delete only `AppData\Local\ValorMortis\Saved\Config\Windows\GameUserSettings.ini` with the game closed. Copy it elsewhere first if you want the old sliders back.
4. Do not delete `Saved`. That folder is the chapter. Chapter 4's post-boss save was a separate fix. Wiping Saved to "make the driver stick" deletes the lantern you just earned.
5. Launch, let the game rewrite the ini, and run one street before you paste old binds back in. Pasting the demo ini back is how the hitch returns.

Error logging was turned down in the same patch because it could cost frames in rare cases. That change is in the update, not in the file you delete. Do both if you are the demo player. Do the driver first if you are everyone else.

## What a new driver will not do

It will not restore a Chapter 4 cutscene that failed to save on the old build. Update, then redo the boss and watch the cutscene through. Confirm the next lantern. The notes do not promise a retroactive flag.

It will not start In the Name of Art if you never loaded Chapter 5 on the patched build. Quest flags are not driver flags.

It will not raise you from the minimum spec to a 4K frame-generation review. Minimum is a Ryzen 5 3600 or i5-10600K, **16 GB** of RAM, an RX 5600 XT or GTX 1660 Ti, and **60 GB** on an SSD, about 30 frames at 1080p on Low with upscaling. Recommended is a Ryzen 7 5800X or i5-13400 and an RX 6800 or RTX 3070 for about 60 frames at 1440p on Medium with upscaling. A **1,771**-minute review described 4K ultra with ray tracing and frame generation near 120 frames. That is one machine. Update the driver, turn upscaling on, and judge the street against the line your GPU is actually on.

## After the street holds

Parry one enemy. If the swing is visible and you miss it, that is timing. If the swing is a hitch, you are still on a disk or a preset problem. The SSD is required. A hard drive will stutter a soulslike even with a fresh driver. Move the install before you delete a second folder.

**39** achievements will survive a driver update. **RookieMistake** at **67.4%** will not pop because the popup went away. It will pop when you die the way the achievement wants, on a build that stays open long enough to count. Send the crash report if you still crash. The notes ask for that. A driver you ignored is a report they have already categorized.

The **$39.99** game is a week old in this note. The first update was a first round. Read the popup. Update the driver. Delete one ini only if the demo left it behind. Then fight. The rapier is the next page, not another folder.

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

Read the popup. Update the driver it names. Then, and only if you came from the playtest or the demo, delete GameUserSettings.ini and nothing above it. Saved is the chapter. Chapter 4's lantern lives there. A fresh driver will not turn a 1660 Ti into a 4K frame-generation clip. It will make the street honest enough to parry. Send a crash report if you still crash. The notes asked. Do the round in order: popup, driver, one ini if you are the demo player, one street, one parry. Folders are not the fight.

One street after the driver update is the proof. If it hitches, then look at the SSD and the ini. If it does not, go parry. The popup already did the hard part by naming the driver.
