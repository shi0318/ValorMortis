The US Steam page on **October 25, 2026** counted **1,060** English reviews for Valor Mortis and labeled them **Very Positive**. The price was **$39.99**. No last-30-days line appeared in the check this page uses. A 30-day percentage is not quoted, because it was not on the page.

Twelve days after the **October 13** launch, 1,060 English reviews is the public argument so far. It is not every language. It is not every owner. It is the line to cite when someone asks what the US store says today.

## What the English page is agreeing on

A first-person parry, a Napoleonic soldier, a blade and a gun. William is that soldier. The reviews that like the game like that split. The reviews that do not are still inside a Very Positive label, which means they are the minority of this English count, not a secret majority. Hitboxes and early save bugs were real. The October 16 patch named the save bugs. The hitboxes are a combat complaint the label did not erase.

Do not turn 1,060 into a tier list. Gun of Glory and Bladesmith stay rare because materials are finite, not because the English page ranked the pistol above the rapier. Parry first. Spend the gun on a window you earned.

Do not turn 1,060 into a reason to skip the driver popup. Outdated drivers were called out in the first update. Update the driver it names. Then, if you came from the demo, delete one settings file. Not the chapter.
