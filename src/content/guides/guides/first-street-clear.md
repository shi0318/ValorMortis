The opening street is the whole combat lesson. **William** has a rapier and a pistol. The parry makes the window. The pistol spends it. Firing during the wind-up means the reload happens while the next swing is already coming.

Install the **First Update** before you decide the window is shorter than the Demo. The Demo is a different app. [First Update](/guides/first-update/).

## Three beats

1. Parry or dodge the swing you can see. [Parry before the shot](/guides/parry-before-the-shot/).
2. Shoot in the recovery. Keep a round if a second body is already on the street.
3. If they bunch up, Fire or Stasis clears the pile faster than a second mag. That is a Transmutation spend, not a new tree. [Three builds](/guides/three-builds-not-one-tree/).

Health and endurance are the early nodes because they buy another parry. A damage node does not.
