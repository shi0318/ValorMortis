Steam's news feed for App 2828710 lists **Valor Mortis - First Update** after the October 13, 2026 launch note. Install that update and restart before you decide a launch-day bug is still in the game.

The storefront date is October 13, 2026. The US price on October 16 is **$39.99** with no discount. Thirty-nine achievements. English reviews are Mostly Positive, 477 positive out of 610. One More Level developed it. The Demo is a separate app, 4641840, and does not inherit this update by itself.

## Retest the retail save

Load the retail save after the update finishes. Do not use a Demo save as the test. Demo Save Anytime and retail Save Anytime are not the same promise. See the [Demo archive](/demo/) only if you are still on that build.

Wishlist counts from launch week are not a patch. The First Update is the patch.
