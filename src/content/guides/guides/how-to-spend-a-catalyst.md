A **Catalyst** is the currency for a skill node or the upgrade that keeps one weapon relevant. It is not a boss key. The loops that earn it are on [Catalyst farming](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/). This page is what to do with the stack in your pocket.

Spend it before you walk away from the lantern. A death reloads the fight. It does not refund a node you never bought, and it does not protect a stack you were saving for a door you have not seen.

## Spend rules

1. Pick one opener: rapier, firearm, or Transmutation. [Three builds](/guides/three-builds-not-one-tree/).
2. Buy endurance or the resource that opener actually uses. A third element is not a spend.
3. If the next room is a boss door, stop farming and use the [checklist](/guides/boss-door-checklist/). Do not respec in the fog.
4. Retail Catalysts are on the full game. A Demo save is a different app.

The First Update can change a yield. It does not change this order.
