**In the Name of Art** is a **Chapter 5** sidequest the first Valor Mortis patch called out by name. The **October 16, 2026** notes say an issue could stop you starting it or progressing it. If the quest did not appear, or appeared and would not advance, install that update before you decide the giver is optional flavor.

The game launched **October 13, 2026**. This was the first update, three days later. On **October 20** Steam listed it at **$39.99**, Mostly Positive, **1,602** positive reviews out of **2,054**. A sidequest bug in the first week is normal. Ignoring the patch and writing a "missable quest" guide is how the bug becomes folklore.

## What the note does and does not say

It says the start and the progress of In the Name of Art on Chapter 5 could break, and that the break is fixed. It does not print the quest steps, the reward, or a map pin. So this page will not invent a route through rooms the notes did not name. The practical guide is the install order.

1. Update to the October 16 build or newer before you go looking for the quest.
2. Enter Chapter 5 on that build. If you already walked the chapter on the launch install and the quest never started, load back to the Chapter 5 start after the patch rather than pushing on and hoping a flag repairs itself. The notes do not promise a retroactive start.
3. Talk to the giver again if you had already talked once on the broken build. A failed start is a failed start. The second conversation on the fixed build is the one that counts.
4. If it starts and then stalls, do not skip ahead to Chapter 12 to "reset the game." Chapter 12 had its own cutscene and lantern blocker in the same patch. Fix the quest you are on.

The [side quest hub](/guides/side-quests/) is where the wider list lives. This page is only the quest the patch named, because that is the one a launch-week player can waste an hour on.

## Do not confuse it with the other first-week bugs

Chapter 4's post-boss save could fail after the cutscene. That is a main-path blocker. [Confirm the lantern](/guides/chapter-4-save-the-cutscene/) before you go hunting optional work. A sidequest will still be there after a saved boss. A boss that did not save will not wait for a painting quest.

Chapter 12's cutscenes could fail to trigger or to unlock the next lantern. If you are not on Chapter 5, you are not on In the Name of Art. Read the chapter number in the menu before you follow a sidequest note into the wrong street.

Volunteer enemies who stopped attacking after a patrol backstab were fixed in the same list. The note jokes that the free kill is over. That change can make Chapter 5 streets feel meaner than a demo memory. It is not the quest failing to start. If the giver talks and the volunteers fight back, both fixes are working. Parry the volunteer. [The parry still comes before the shot](/guides/parry-before-the-shot/).

Stutters on a demo or playtest settings file are a third issue. Delete `GameUserSettings.ini` if that is you. Do not delete it to make a quest appear. An ini is not a quest flag.

## How long to spend on it

A sidequest that would not start is worth one patched attempt, not a night of reloads on the October 13 build. If it starts, play it. If the steps are unclear, write down the objective text the game shows and follow that text. A guide that invents a reward the patch did not mention will waste the next player the same way the bug did.

**39** achievements are on the Steam listing. **RookieMistake** was **67.4%** on October 20. **TheBookOfBeasts** was **7.4%**. Nothing in the October 16 note ties In the Name of Art to either pop. If the in-game achievement list names the quest, follow that list after the quest actually starts. Do not assume a 7% achievement is a painting because the numbers are both small.

Minimum spec remains **16 GB** of RAM, a GTX 1660 Ti or RX 5600 XT, and **60 GB** on an SSD. A quest trigger is not a frame-rate problem. If the giver's scene hitch is so hard you cannot tell whether the quest started, then yes, fix the driver and the old settings file first. Otherwise leave the graphics menu alone and check the quest log.

The first update asked for crash reports and promised more fixes. This quest was already on the list they closed. Start it on the build that closed it. Then go back to the street.

Chapter 5 is also a combat chapter, and the same patch took away a free kill. Volunteers who had been backstabbed on patrol could stop attacking. That is fixed. If the quest giver finally talks and the next patrol suddenly fights, both lines of the update are working. Do not abandon In the Name of Art because the street got honest. Parry the volunteer, spend the pistol on a recovery you already earned, and keep the quest log open so you can see the objective advance. A fight and a stall are different. The log is how you tell them apart.

The **$39.99** price and the Mostly Positive score do not tell you the reward. The in-game objective does. Write it down if you have to leave the chair. A week-old game can still change a sidequest's wording in the next patch, and the notes said more fixes are coming. What should not change is the habit: patched build, Chapter 5, giver, log. Launch-day folklore about a missable painting is how the start bug survived in guides after the studio had already closed it.

If the giver's scene stutters so hard you cannot hear the offer, you are allowed to fix performance first. Demo and playtest players were told to delete one settings file, not the save. Do that, update the driver if the new popup asks, and return to Chapter 5. A smooth scene on a deleted quest flag helps nobody. Keep the save. Fix the ini only if you are the player the note addressed. Then start the quest the patch made startable, and leave the invented route to someone who has actually finished it on this build.
