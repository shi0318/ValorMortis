**Catalysts** pay for skills and for the upgrade that keeps a rapier or a Transmutation relevant. The fast loop is the one beside a lantern. Crossing half a chapter for a larger pack is slower than two short resets.

Spend before you leave. A lantern is the safe point. A boss door is not a farm. If the enemy on that loop no longer pays for the time, move to the next lantern. The four loops are on the [Catalyst farming guide](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/).

## What not to do

- Do not respec in the middle of a boss attempt to chase a farm number. Pick the path first: rapier, firearm, or Transmutation.
- Do not use a Demo save as the farm file. Retail Catalysts are on the full game.
- Fire and Stasis clear a grouped loop. They do not replace the parry on a single heavy swing.

Yields move when the game is patched. The First Update is the build to retest a loop that suddenly pays less.
