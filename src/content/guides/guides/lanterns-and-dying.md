Valor Mortis is a first-person soulslike from the Ghostrunner studio, and it keeps the genre's central bargain: **Lanterns are safety, and safety resets the world**. Understanding that one trade is most of the difficulty curve.

## What a Lantern does

- **Restores Ferro Flask charges.** Your healing is finite between rests.
- **Resets the enemies around it.** That is the cost of resting — and also the reason farming works at all.
- **Banks your Catalysts** when you spend them. Catalysts in your pocket are at risk; Catalysts spent on a skill node are not.
- **Acts as fast travel** once that unlocks after the Chapter 2 Lothaire fight; before then, Lanterns are rest points only. Fast travel is what makes side-quest cleanup practical.

## The rule that saves runs

**Spend before you explore.** The most common expensive death in the game is dying with a full pocket of unspent Catalysts on the way to somewhere interesting. If you have enough for a node, buy the node at the Lantern you are standing at — even a node that was not your first choice. A bought node cannot be lost.

The same applies before a boss door: rest, refill, spend, then open it.

## There is no difficulty selector

The game ships without difficulty options. What that means in practice:

1. **Your build is the difficulty slider.** Health and Endurance first, damage later — the [skill priority list](/guides/best-skills-guide/) is the order.
2. **Flask charges are the second slider.** Arriving at a fight with two charges instead of full is a self-imposed hard mode.
3. **The farm is the third.** If a chapter is beating you, the answer is usually a Lantern loop and two more skill nodes rather than better execution.

## Farm with the reset, not against it

Because resting respawns the enemies beside a Lantern, the loop is always the same shape: clear the group, walk back, rest, turn around. The Chapter 1 Onslaught Arch route works before you have any upgrades; Under the Bridge in Chapter 5 and the Fortress Chapel in Chapter 10 are the single-target versions. Full routes are on the [Catalyst farming page](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/).

Measure a loop in **Catalysts per minute over five full resets**, not per kill. A richer enemy that is two minutes away is worse than a poor one beside the Lantern.

## Three achievements that are just the loop

Three of the most common achievements in the game are things that happen to everyone at a Lantern: **Rookie Mistake** (press flask with none left, **62.9%**), **In the Nick of Time** (reach a Lantern below 20% health with no flasks, **50.6%**), and **Mind the Gap** (die by falling into the abyss, **56.0%**). If you want them deliberately, bank your Catalysts first.
