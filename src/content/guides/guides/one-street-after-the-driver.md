The October 16 update added a popup when the GPU driver is old. The notes said outdated drivers were one of the largest stability gaps in the first days. Update that driver. Walk one street. Parry one enemy. That is the test. Deleting folders is not the test.

On **October 25, 2026** the US page was **$39.99**, Very Positive, **1,089** English reviews. No 30-day split in this check. Twelve days after launch, a Very Positive English count does not mean your driver is current. The popup is how the game tells you.

## The order

Install the October 16 build or newer. If the popup names a driver, update it and restart. Launch. Walk the boulevard in the screenshot, or any street you can already reach. If it holds, parry one swing with the rapier. If the swing is visible and you miss it, that is timing. If the swing is a hitch, you are still on a disk or a preset problem.

The SSD is required. **60 GB**. A hard drive will stutter a first-person parry even with a fresh driver. Move the install before you delete a second folder. Minimum spec is a GTX 1660 Ti or RX 5600 XT and **16 GB** of RAM, about 30 frames at 1080p on Low with upscaling. A 4K frame-generation clip is someone else's machine. Turn upscaling on. Judge the street against the line your GPU is on.

Delete `GameUserSettings.ini` only if you played the playtest or the demo and the street still stutters after the driver update. Only that file, under `Config\Windows`. Do not delete `Saved`. Chapter 4's lantern and Chapter 12's lantern live in the save. A performance tip that eats the chapter is not the note the studio printed.

## What one street proves

It proves the driver and the frame, not the whole game. Volunteers should fight back after a patrol backstab on this patch. The pistol will not freeze them. The rapier should answer the swing. In the Name of Art should be started on this build if you are in Chapter 5 and it never appeared. Those are separate tests. Do them after the street holds, not instead of it.

**$39.99** is the sticker. **1,089** Very Positive English reviews are the crowd who got far enough to write. Some of them updated the driver. Some did not. You can tell which you are. Send a crash report if you still crash. The notes asked. **39** achievements survive a driver update. They do not pop because the popup went away.

One street. One parry. Then the chapter you were actually playing. The store page will not walk the boulevard. The driver might let you see it.
