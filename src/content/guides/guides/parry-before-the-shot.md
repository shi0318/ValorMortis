**Valor Mortis** gives William a rapier and a pistol in the same encounter. The parry is the window. The shot is how you spend it. Firing first, then trying to parry the recovery, is how a Mostly Positive launch file becomes a death on the first street.

Endurance and health are the early skill picks because they buy more parries and dodges, not because they raise a hidden gun tier. Ammo capacity matters after you can survive the swing that follows a miss. See [best skills](/guides/best-skills-guide/).

## One encounter

1. Parry or dodge the swing you can see.
2. Shoot in the recovery, not during the wind-up.
3. If the enemy is grouped, a Transmutation clears the pile faster than a second pistol mag. Fire and Stasis are the tools on the [Catalyst page](/guides/catalyst-farming-guide/) when the loop is a lantern, not a boss.

Install the First Update before you decide the parry window is shorter than the Demo. The Demo is a different app.
