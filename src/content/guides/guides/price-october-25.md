The US Steam page on **October 25, 2026** listed Valor Mortis at **$39.99**. No discount was shown. English reviews were Very Positive, **1,060** for the game. The launch was **October 13**. Twelve days of full price next to a Very Positive English page is the offer. A sale is not hiding in the review label.

This check did not print a 30-day review split. The price page does not need one. The button was **$39.99**. The label was Very Positive. That is enough to decide, and not enough to invent a percentage.

## What the price includes

William, a blade, a gun, and a first-person parry, from One More Level. The October 16 update if you install it: driver detection, a Chapter 4 save fix, a Chapter 12 lantern fix, and a Chapter 5 quest fix. An SSD requirement and **60 GB**. Not a second protagonist. Not a free pistol meta. Not a settings wipe that keeps your chapter.

Minimum spec is a GTX 1660 Ti or RX 5600 XT and **16 GB** of RAM, about 30 frames at 1080p on Low with upscaling. **$39.99** does not upgrade that card. A driver popup is free. Read it. A 4K frame-generation clip in a review is someone else's machine.

## Buy, wait, or update

Buy if the parry is what you want and **$39.99** is acceptable twelve days after launch. 1,060 Very Positive English reviews are the crowd. They are not a coupon.

Wait if you need a discount. The page can grow one. It had not on October 25.

Update if you own it and Chapter 4 did not save. Watch the cutscene. Confirm the lantern. Do not delete the save folder to celebrate a price check. Delete one ini only if a demo left it behind, and only after the driver update.

The rapier still comes before the pistol. Materials are finite. The store price does not refill them. **$39.99** is the number until the button prints another one. Very Positive is the company. The swing is still the test.
