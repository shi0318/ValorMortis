The **rapier** makes the window. The **pistol** spends it. Valor Mortis lets William hold both, and the first-week reviews like that split. A **1,551**-minute review praised the left ranged hand and the right melee hand. A **1,771**-minute review called the combat parry-focused. Neither review said to max the gun before you can parry. **Gun of Glory**, one firearm maxed, was around **6.5%**. **Bladesmith**, one melee weapon maxed, was around **8.7%**. Both are rare because materials are finite. Spend the first pile on the blade you parry with.

The game launched **October 13, 2026**, at **$39.99**. On **October 20** reviews were Mostly Positive, **1,602** of **2,054**. A week-old soulslike does not have a public DPS table that proves the pistol outscales the rapier. Play the hand the parry is on.

## The order

1. Parry one street enemy with the rapier until the timing is boring. If you cannot see the swing, fix the frame before you buy a gun upgrade. Demo players delete `GameUserSettings.ini` if the street stutters. That file is settings, not a weapon.
2. Put the next upgrade into the rapier, not a second element and not the pistol, until one punish feels reliable.
3. Use the pistol on the recovery you already earned. A shot that opens the fight is a shot you fired instead of a parry.
4. Stasis and fire are for the pack, after the single swing is answered. Do not buy both in the first hour. [Three builds](/guides/three-builds-not-one-tree/) is the spend page.
5. Max the pistol when Gun of Glory is a choice you can afford, not when a search said best gun.

Volunteer enemies no longer stop attacking after a patrol backstab. The October 16 note closed that free kill. The rapier still has to answer the swing they throw after they wake up. The pistol will not freeze them. The patch removed the freeze.

## What the pistol is for

It is for the body that is already staggered, or the recovery you would waste on a second rapier poke you do not have time for. It is not armor. It is not a second protagonist. William is one soldier. The Grande Armée framing on the store page is one pair of hands.

Catalysts and Transmutations are the other wallet. [How to spend a Catalyst](/guides/how-to-spend-a-catalyst/) matters more than a pistol attachment if you have not committed to a tree. A gun upgrade on an empty power budget is a cosmetic with a reload.

Chapter 4's post-boss save was fixed on October 16. If you are upgrading weapons to avoid redoing that boss, update first and confirm the lantern. A lost cutscene is not a rapier problem. A Chapter 5 quest that will not start is not a pistol problem. In the Name of Art is a quest flag. Fix the flag on the patched build. Then go back to the street and parry.

## Numbers, not a tier list

**39** achievements. **RookieMistake** at **67.4%** is the early death, not a weapon rank. **DrumSolo** at **60.3%** is not a gun tier. **TheBookOfBeasts** at **7.4%** is in the same neighborhood as the max-weapon pops, which is a hint that commitment is rare, not a hint that the book replaces the rapier.

Minimum spec is a GTX 1660 Ti or RX 5600 XT, **16 GB** of RAM, and **60 GB** on an SSD, about 30 frames at 1080p on Low with upscaling. A parry you miss because the frame hitched is not a reason to main the pistol. Update the driver if the October 16 popup says it is old. Then parry again.

The **$39.99** pitch is Napoleon, first person, blade and gun. Keep the blade as the clock. Let the gun be the punctuation. Players who finished and called it an 8 are describing that clock. Players who bounced off hitboxes are not describing a secret pistol meta. Hitboxes were a complaint in a **1,386**-minute positive review. A gun upgrade will not fix a box. A parry you can see might route around it. Learn the rapier. Spend the pistol when the window is already yours.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.

The first materials go to the hand that makes the window. Gun of Glory at about 6.5 percent can wait. Bladesmith at about 8.7 percent is the commitment the rapier wants first, and even that can wait until the parry is boring. Volunteer enemies fight back after the October 16 patch. The pistol will not freeze them. A hitch is a driver or an ini, not a reason to main the gun. Update, see the swing, parry it, then spend a shot on the recovery. A best-gun search is a later evening. This evening is the blade.
