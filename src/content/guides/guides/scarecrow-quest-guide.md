The **Scarecrow** is a Chapter 3 side quest in the **Fallen Village**. Its start is the scarecrow post near the **Fields Lantern**, and the quest rewards the **Bountiful Harvest Amber**. The useful part of the route is not just finding the start: the reward is connected to an encounter in the barn, so follow the quest through before pushing deeper into the village.

This guide keeps the Scarecrow route separate from the nearby **Lullaby** quest and from the chapter’s main path. Fallen Village is dense with houses, a marketplace, a barn, and optional supplies. If you treat every side objective as one long quest, it is easy to miss the scarecrow trigger or confuse its completion with the crib objective.

## Start at the Fields Lantern

Reach Chapter 3 and locate the Fields Lantern in the Fallen Village. The Scarecrow begins at the scarecrow post near that Lantern. Interact with the post to start the chain, then read the objective the game adds. Do not assume that finding the barn by itself starts the quest; the marked post is the important start point.

The easiest order is to pick up the quest when you first pass the Lantern, before you clear every side room. That gives you a reason to investigate the barn while you are already exploring the village. The existing Chapter 3 walkthrough places the Scarecrow at this Fields Lantern start and identifies the barn as the objective location.

Before leaving the Lantern area, check your health and flask charges. The quest takes you into a combat space with enemies and hazards. The route is more predictable if you approach with healing available, rather than after spending everything in the main path.

## Follow the barn objective

The quest’s barn sequence takes place at the **Fallen Village Marketplace**. The walkthrough identifies a barn full of explosive bodies and places the **Volatile End Amber** at the back right. It also says to climb to the top of the barn and use the two pillows to finish the quest.

Use the objective text as your confirmation that the chain is active. Enter the barn, scan the room before committing to a fight, and note the back-right Amber position. The enemy group is packed closely, which is why the barn also appears in Catalyst farming routes. If you are here for the quest, prioritize finishing its objective and securing the Amber rather than turning the room into a long farming session.

The practical sequence is:

1. Start the Scarecrow quest at the scarecrow post near the Fields Lantern.
2. Follow the quest objective toward the Fallen Village Marketplace barn.
3. Clear the encounter carefully and collect the Volatile End Amber at the back right.
4. Climb to the top of the barn.
5. Use the two pillows as described by the objective walkthrough to complete the chain.
6. Check the quest state or reward notification before moving on.

If the game does not show the quest objective, go back to the post and confirm you interacted with the correct start. If the Amber is missing after the fight, search the back-right part of the barn before you leave. The Amber and the quest finish are both easy to overlook when you sprint through the room.

## Fight through the barn without wasting resources

The barn can group several Shambling Civilians close together. Entering with your pistol empty or your healing depleted makes a simple cleanup feel harder than it needs to be. Use a Lantern first if you are nearby and spend any carried Catalysts before taking a risky route; death before recovery can cost the unbanked stack.

The Catalyst farming guide recommends the Marketplace barn as a repeatable group encounter and describes using Fire when enemies funnel through the doorway. That is a farming option, not a required quest tactic. For a first quest clear, use the tool you control best and keep an exit lane. Do not cast into a cluster if you need to preserve a specific target or avoid igniting the wrong area.

A short range plan keeps the encounter manageable: pull enemies into a space where you can see them, avoid being surrounded, and use a parry or dodge to create room. The pistol is better spent during a recovery than during an enemy’s active wind-up. If the group overwhelms you, retreat to the approach and thin them out rather than trying to finish the quest while cornered.

## Keep the Scarecrow separate from Lullaby

Fallen Village also contains **Lullaby**, a separate side quest that begins at the empty crib near the Marketplace. Its reward is **Tomorrow’s Promise Amber**, and the Chapter 3 walkthrough says to put the Toy in the crib. That is not the Scarecrow completion step.

Use the landmarks to distinguish them:

- **Scarecrow:** scarecrow post near the Fields Lantern; barn sequence; Bountiful Harvest Amber.
- **Lullaby:** empty crib near the Marketplace; Toy interaction; Tomorrow’s Promise Amber.

Both chains can be handled while exploring the same chapter, but do not use the crib objective to verify the Scarecrow quest. Finish one, confirm its own reward or quest state, then continue with the other if you want a full side-content sweep.

## What to do if the quest does not complete

If the objective remains active after you climb the barn, review the sequence before leaving the chapter area:

- Did you activate the post near the Fields Lantern first?
- Did you reach the Marketplace barn named by the objective?
- Did you collect the Volatile End Amber from the back-right part of the barn?
- Did you climb to the top and interact with both pillows?
- Did the game display a completion change or reward notice?

The guide material identifies these landmarks and actions, but it does not describe a hidden fail timer, a specific dialog line, or a choice that locks the chain. Do not conclude the quest is permanently missed because one objective marker is unclear. Revisit the start and the barn, read the on-screen quest text, and verify the two interactions before advancing.

## Use the Amber as a completion check

The Scarecrow reward is the **Bountiful Harvest Amber**. When it appears in your inventory or the game confirms the pickup, record that state before you move on. Ambers are useful for progression and achievement cleanup, and an unrecorded pickup can make a later completion route harder to audit.

The 100% guide recommends completing side quests as soon as they open and sweeping Amber routes alongside them. That advice applies here: pick up the quest early, take the nearby optional route while you are in the village, and confirm the Amber rather than assuming the quest completion automatically added it. If you are following a completion checklist, mark the quest and the Amber separately.

## Short route summary

Start at the scarecrow post near the Fields Lantern in Chapter 3’s Fallen Village. Follow the active quest to the Marketplace barn, clear the encounter, collect the Volatile End Amber at the back right, climb to the top, and use the two pillows. Verify the completion or reward before you leave. Do not confuse this chain with Lullaby at the empty crib.
