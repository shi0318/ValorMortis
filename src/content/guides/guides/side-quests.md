Valor Mortis hides a lot of its content in quest chains that start from a single NPC or object. None of them is announced, and an NPC that moves on while you push the main story is how a completion run gets ruined. Start them when you find them.

Community trackers count **20 to 24 side quests** in total. This page covers the chains that carry an achievement or an Amber reward, which are the ones worth planning around.

## The quest chains by chapter

| Quest | Starts in | Reward or achievement | Steam rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Oathbound** | Chapter 2, then the Battlefield with the Pistol: an Eternal Guardian behind a weak-point wall near Onslaught Arch | We Wear the Same Colors (with every other Eternal Guardist) | 2.1% |
| **An Unheard Farewell** | Chapter 2, Valley of Ashes — agree to help the Adjudant Sous-Officier | Aggressive Reconnaissance | 52.1% |
| **The Emissary** | Chapter 2, the poisoned passage near The Other Side | Cannot be finished until you have Shield | — |
| **The Scarecrow** | Chapter 3, Fallen Village — the scarecrow post near the Fields Lantern | Bountiful Harvest Amber | — |
| **Lullaby** | Chapter 3, Fallen Village — the empty crib near the Marketplace | Tomorrow's Promise Amber | — |
| **The Deserter** | Chapter 5, Vale of Wrath — a note in a ruined hut near the Under the Bridge Lantern | Hand in the Jar | — |
| **The Curious Case of Seamus Diggory** | Chapter 6, Cursed Hollow | Blood and Wine | 10.0% |
| **Laudanum** | Chapter 7, the Outskirts | The Bitter Cure | — |
| **The Monster Hunt** | Chapter 8 — the drawing on a tree at the start of the chapter | The Book of Beasts | 2.7% |

## Read the rates

**Aggressive Reconnaissance** at 52.1% is the one most people stumble into — it starts on the main route through the Valley of Ashes. Everything after that falls off a cliff: **The Book of Beasts** at 2.7% and **We Wear the Same Colors** at 2.1% are two of the five rarest achievements in the game, and neither is hard. They are just easy to walk past.

## The Oathbound is the one to plan

**We Wear the Same Colors** asks you to defeat all Eternal Guardists, which means finishing The Oathbound **and** revisiting the Guardist arenas you skipped. Arenas are spread across the campaign, so a cleanup pass at the end is a long one — tick them off as you reach them.

## A working order

1. **Start a quest the moment it appears.** Lantern fast travel makes returning cheap; a vanished NPC does not.
2. **Finish the chain before you push the next chapter**, particularly in Chapters 5 to 8 where several chains overlap. The Deserter needs three items from beehives: search only hives **without** a red X, because marked hives release angry bees.
3. **Sweep side paths for Ambers in the same pass.** The quest routes and the Amber routes are mostly the same side paths.
4. **Save the combat feats for last.** Quests and exploration first, weapon upgrades second, challenge achievements third, story bosses last.

If a marker is gone, use the chapter route and Lantern fast travel before you consider restarting — the chain is usually still completable from a later point.
