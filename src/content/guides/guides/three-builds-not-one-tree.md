The skill board is survival, endurance, Nephtoglobin, firearm, and damage. The opening that survives a street is not a pure damage rush. Health, endurance, and ammo decide how many parries, dodges, and shots fit in one fight.

Pick one spender. A rapier build wants the parry window and the follow-up. A firearm build wants ammo before a second damage node. A Transmutation build wants the resource to cast Fire or Stasis when the pack is grouped. Mixing all three in the first hour is how you arrive at a boss with no loop.

## The split

| Opener | First spends | Do not buy first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rapier | Health, endurance, the parry follow-up | A gun node you will not fire |
| Firearm | Ammo, then the shot that spends a parry window | Damage that does not refill the mag |
| Transmutation | The cast you already use on lantern loops | A third element you have not tried |

The ranked list and the three paths are on [best skills](/guides/best-skills-guide/). This page is the rule: one opener, endurance before greed. Retail numbers can move after the First Update. Do not copy a Demo node onto the full board.
