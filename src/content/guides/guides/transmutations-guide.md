Transmutations are Valor Mortis's power set, and they run on **Nephtoglobin** — the plague substance that is also in William's blood. That shared fiction is the design: the thing killing the continent is your ammunition.

## The powers and what each one is for

There are **three Transmutations**: **Fire** (Chapter 1), **Stasis** and **Shield**. The Flamethrower, Shield Bash and Nephtowall are abilities that come with them as you progress.

| Transmutation | Job | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Area damage and ignition | Three or more enemies funnel through a door or corridor |
| **Flamethrower** | Sustained close ignition | A tight group you can hold the input on — four at once is the **Flame Feast** achievement |
| **Stasis** | Lock a single target | A heavy enemy or a boss weak point you need a clean second on |
| **Shield / Shield Bash** | Deflect and displace | A ledge fight, or a swing you cannot parry |
| **Nephtowall** | Traversal and vantage | Getting above an enemy for an airborne shot |

**Fire is for groups, Stasis is for one thing.** That is the whole decision most of the time. Fire does not replace a parry on a single heavy swing, and Stasis wasted on a crowd leaves you empty when the real target arrives.

## The Nephtoglobin economy

The resource does not refill on its own unless you equip the **Bottomless Well** Amber from Chapter 1, which restores it slowly. Otherwise you take it from the world and from kills, and that means every cast is a choice about the next thirty seconds, not the next fight.

1. **Raise Maximum Nephtoglobin before Transmutation Damage.** A bigger pool is more casts; more damage per cast is worthless if you only get one.
2. **Nephtoglobin Sufferance** is the bridge node: it makes a normal melee build able to afford an emergency Fire or Stasis without committing to a full power build.
3. **Do not open an encounter with a cast.** Open with the firearm or a parry, see how the group bunches, then spend.
4. **Melee is the backup.** If you are dry, fight with the blade and firearm until the pool recovers rather than waiting for a cast.

## Two achievements that teach the system

**Statue of Insanity** — fully lock a Remnant of the Iron Regiment in Stasis — is held by **7.3%** of players, and the Under the Bridge Lantern in Chapter 5 is the best place to practise it because the Remnant resets on a short loop. **Flame Feast** (four enemies ignited with one Flamethrower use, **16.2%**) works in the Chapter 3 Marketplace barn, where six Shambling Civilians funnel through a single doorway.

Both are the same lesson: the powers are strongest where the level geometry does the grouping for you.

## Upgrade one, not three

**Transmutation Artifex** asks for one Transmutation fully upgraded and sits at **5.4%**. Materials are finite enough that spreading them across every power leaves you with three mediocre ones at the Chapter 7 double boss. Pick the power that matches your melee weapon — Fire with a crowd-clearing Claymore, Stasis with a precise Rapier — and finish it.
