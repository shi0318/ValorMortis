The October 16 note closed a free kill. Volunteer enemies could stop attacking after being backstabbed while on patrol. They should not, on the patched build. The note joked that the free kills were over. If your route still assumes a backstab switches their AI off, retest it. Parry the swing they throw after they wake.

On **October 25, 2026** the US page was **$39.99**, Very Positive, **1,089** English reviews, no 30-day split in this check. A friendly label does not restore the bug. It means most English reviewers recommended the game anyway, including the week the free kill disappeared.

## Retest one patrol

Update. Find a Volunteer on a patrol you already know. Backstab. Do not stand in the recovery waiting for them to idle. If they swing, the fix held. Parry with the rapier. The pistol spends that window. It does not freeze them. Stasis is for a pack, not for hoping the old bug returns.

If they still idle, confirm the build. A launch-hour install will still show the free kill. Do not write a guide around it. Do not drop difficulty because they fight. Difficulty is damage. The patch gave them their attack back. That is the intended street.

## One soldier, one answer

William holds the blade and the gun. Max the rapier until the parry is boring. Gun of Glory can wait. A Volunteer who fights back is not a reason to respec into the pistol. Hitboxes were a complaint inside positive reviews. A gun upgrade will not fix a box. A parry you can see might route around it. If the street hitches, update the driver the popup names before you call the swing unfair.

Chapter 4's lantern and Chapter 12's lantern are save checks, not this patrol. In the Name of Art is a Chapter 5 quest flag. Do not abandon a sidequest because the next patrol got honest. Both lines of the October 16 update can be working at once: the quest starts, and the Volunteer swings.

## After the swing

You play the street the patch intended. **1,089** Very Positive reviews are not a vote to bring the bug back. **$39.99** does not include a stealth switch that turns patrols off. **39** achievements. The common ones are early deaths and first steps. A free kill was never the rare pop you should have been farming.

Minimum spec is **16 GB** and a 1660 Ti-class card, **60 GB** on an SSD. A stutter that makes the wake-up swing invisible is a frame problem. Fix the driver and the disk. Then parry. The free kill is the old game. The October 25 page is selling the one where they fight back.
