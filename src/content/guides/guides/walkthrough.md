The full game of Valor Mortis runs **eleven chapters plus a final region, Entropy**. Each chapter is one region of a plague-ruined 19th-century Europe, and eight of them end behind a named boss. This page is the order. The per-chapter pages linked below cover the route, the Lanterns and every pickup.

## How the campaign is shaped

**Chapters 1–3 are the tutorial arc.** You leave Chapter 3 with the full early kit: Saber, Pistol, Rapier, Blunderbuss, the Fire Transmutation and the Grapple. Nothing in these three chapters is missable, and fast travel between Lanterns opens once the Lothaire fight is behind you.

**Chapters 4 and 5 are the wall.** The Sphinx is the first boss where a pure damage build stops working, and the Slaughtermill punishes contact harder than anything before it. If a run stalls, it is usually here. Put early skill points into Health and Endurance before you open the Caverns. The [best skills guide](/guides/best-skills-guide/) has the order.

**Chapter 7 is two fights in one chapter.** High Justicar Aurelius and Experiment 83 both sit in the Outskirts. Rest between them; the second fight comes soon after the first.

**Chapters 8 and 11 are breathers in boss terms, not in difficulty.** The City of Orphans has no boss but sets up the Orphan in Chapter 9. Napoleon's Prison has only two Lanterns and leads straight into Entropy.

**The finale reuses everything.** The Entity, Voice of the Void brings back attacks from the bosses before it, so the last fight is a review of every red, unblockable attack you have learned to dodge.

## Revisiting chapters

Every region stays open after you finish it. Several routes are gated by abilities you get later. The Grapple from Chapter 3 opens upper ledges in the Valley of Ashes, and a poisoned passage near The Other Side needs an ability from much later in the campaign. If a chest or path looks unreachable, note the Lantern and come back. Do not grind the jump.
