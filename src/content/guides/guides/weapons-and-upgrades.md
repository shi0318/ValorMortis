Valor Mortis gives William a blade, a gun and a power at the same time, and the mistake almost everyone makes is treating that as three budgets. It is one budget. Upgrade materials are finite, and the achievement list is built around committing: **Bladesmith** (one melee weapon maxed) is at **8.7%**, **Gun of Glory** (one firearm maxed) at **6.5%**, **Transmutation Artifex** at **5.4%**.

## The melee weapons

| Weapon | Where you get it | Defence | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Saber** | Chapter 1, you start with it | Block and parry | The all-rounder, and the safest blade to learn on |
| **Rapier** | Chapter 3, on the path from the Abandoned Church | Parry only | Parry builds; it is the weapon **Symphony of Steel** is designed around |
| **Claymore** | Chapter 6, Cursed Hollow | Block only | Groups and armour — **Hoodsnatcher** asks for three decapitations in one swing |

A missed parry costs Endurance twice: once to defend and again to escape. That is why **Maximum Endurance** sits second in the [skill priority list](/guides/best-skills-guide/), above any melee damage node.

## The firearms

| Firearm | Where you get it | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pistol** | Chapter 2, Valley of Ashes | The airborne-aim gun. **Windhover** wants three kills while Airborne Aiming |
| **Blunderbuss** | Chapter 3, Fallen Village | The hardest-hitting gun; deletes a weak point at close range. **Scattershot** wants five enemies in one shot |
| **Musket** | Chapter 5, Vale of Wrath | The safe opening against anything that punishes melee approach |

Firearm ammunition is limited, so guns are not your main damage source.

Guns are not a second damage build — they are how you **create** an opening. Weak-point shots expose deathblows, and the Blunderbuss weak-point sequence on the Remnant of the Iron Regiment is the core of the best mid-game farm.

## Upgrades at the Abandoned Church

The Abandoned Church is where materials become levels. Check it whenever a new upgrade option appears — **Arsenal Absolute** (unlock every weapon and Transmutation, **6.6%**) is mostly a matter of not walking past it.

**The one-weapon rule:** commit materials to a single blade, a single gun and a single power until each is maxed. Three fully upgraded items beat six half-upgraded ones at every boss door, and it is also the cheapest path to the three smith achievements.

## Ambers

Ambers are the passive layer, picked up from side paths rather than bought. The **Surplus Amber** improves Catalyst gains from kills, which makes every farming loop in the game faster — find it before you commit to a long grind.

**Ambersmith** (gather every Amber) sits at **3.7%**. The reason is traversal: some Ambers sit behind routes that only open once you have a new mobility power, so sweep a chapter again after the power that unblocks it rather than assuming you missed nothing.
