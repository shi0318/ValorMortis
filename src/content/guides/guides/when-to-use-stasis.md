**Stasis** is the Transmutation you spend when more than one body is already in the swing. It is not the opener. The rapier still makes the window. The pistol still spends a single recovery. Stasis spends the resource on the pack.

Fire does the same job when the pack is the problem and you want damage instead of a stop. Do not buy both in the first hour. [Three builds](/guides/three-builds-not-one-tree/).

## Use it when

- The lantern loop has enemies grouped. [Lantern farm](/guides/lantern-loop-not-a-boss/).
- A street flank is already on you and the parry only answered one of them.
- You have the Catalyst spend already committed to this tree. [How to spend a Catalyst](/guides/how-to-spend-a-catalyst/).

Do not cast it at a boss door you have not checked. One opener. Endurance before a second element.
