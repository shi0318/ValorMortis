For Captain Raphael and the Entity, build the attempt around openings you can recognize repeatedly. Do not rely on a clip where Raphael stops attacking after a Rapier heavy hit or where a power appears to persist indefinitely. The October updates explicitly repaired those interactions. A current strategy must still work when the boss continues its normal actions.

Players describe these late fights very differently. In the Steam discussion “difficulty spike,” some find Raphael an endurance test, while others struggle most with the Entity's later combo sequences. Those reports help identify decisions to practise; they do not establish a universal difficulty ranking, a required weapon or a completion rate for players who reached the encounter.

## Prepare for the failure you are actually having

Before changing equipment, identify the action that ends your useful attempt. Being hit during a follow-up, running out of recovery, losing track of the boss and crashing are separate problems. If you can already defend the opening but keep taking one extra swing, a shorter punish is a clearer first change than replacing the entire loadout.

Inspect the recovery charges and upgrades shown in your current game. The October 21 patch fixed a case where upgrading the Ferrofluid Flask might not be saved. If an upgrade appears missing after loading, check that state rather than assuming the fight secretly reduces your supplies. The patch does not establish a compulsory flask count for either boss.

Keep a weapon you understand available while trying an alternative. Player success with a Rapier or Claymore is evidence that their approach worked for them, not proof that your current weapon cannot finish the encounter. Choose an alternative to solve a specific problem such as an overlong commitment or a defensive routine you cannot maintain.

## Captain Raphael: respect the return attack

The October 21 notes adjusted Raphael's ability to block and stagger, and made his strafe, walk and shoot speeds more consistent. Those changes matter when interpreting an older fight video: an opening learned from a different stagger interaction may no longer leave the same amount of time.

Begin with a short attack after a recovery you recognize. Watch whether Raphael resumes movement, blocks, or starts another action before adding a follow-up. If you are repeatedly hit after your own second attack, test ending the punish earlier. This is an observation-based adjustment, not a claim that the boss has a fixed one-hit or two-hit rule.

Do not let a successful stagger become permission to stop reading the fight. The October 27 update fixed Raphael becoming inactive when a Rapier heavy attack staggered him at a particular moment. A video built around leaving him motionless is demonstrating the repaired bug, rather than a dependable current technique.

For an endurance problem, compare how much recovery you spend reaching the same stage of the fight. A slower approach that preserves supplies can be more useful than an aggressive opening followed by repeated emergency healing. You do not need to reproduce another player's reported attempt length or potion count to evaluate that improvement.

## The Entity: learn where the long sequence ends

Reports in the difficulty discussion focus on sustained combo pressure in the later phase. One player prefers a different defensive approach for that sequence instead of continuing the same Rapier parry routine throughout. Another finds maintaining the sequence tiring. These observations support separating the later pattern from the opening rather than assuming the entire fight has one rhythm.

When you reach the unfamiliar sequence, prioritize recognizing its end and keeping enough room for your next defense. Do not attack merely because one strike has passed if the next action is still beginning. Once you can recognize a recovery, try a short punish and check how control returns to you. Expand it only when the result remains readable.

If the visual effects make you lose the immediate threat, reset your view before using another power. The official notes refer to the Entity's recruited bosses and spawned enemies, so another active threat or effect may be relevant. That does not mean every remaining action can be explained by one summon, or that this page provides a verified complete attack catalogue.

## Separate power behavior from old bugs

The Entity received several specific fixes. Use them to interpret the symptom you see, rather than treating every surprising interaction as a new mechanic.

| Observation or old video | Relevant documented change | Decision for a current attempt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stasis behaves differently or persists unexpectedly | October 21 fixed its application, behavior and persistence on the Entity | Check the live result instead of copying an unlimited-duration tactic |
| Fire continues from spawned enemies | October 27 repaired a persistence bug in this fight | Distinguish a continuing normal attack from the repaired indefinite effect |
| The shield appears misplaced | October 27 fixed the Entity shield being in the wrong position | Report a repeatable mismatch instead of inventing a special weak point |
| A teleport seems to remove the boss | October 21 fixed rare disappearance while aimed at during teleport | Preserve the symptom and installation details if it recurs |

None of those notes publishes a universal damage multiplier or a guaranteed Stasis duration. If you add a power to your plan, observe its effect and retain a defensive fallback. A power failing to reproduce a pre-patch exploit is not sufficient evidence that the save or weapon is broken.

## A missing reward is different from losing the fight

The October 21 patch addressed progression being blocked when Raphael and the player died simultaneously. The October 27 notes added further simultaneous-death fixes and say affected missing boss achievements or trophies should unlock by pressing **Continue** on the main menu.

Update first, use Continue, and check both the campaign state and the platform achievement list. Do not deliberately recreate the trade kill or replace your save simply to trigger a notification. If the issue persists, the [boss and chapter recovery guide](/boss-achievement-and-chapter-recovery/) explains the evidence to preserve and the limits of a replacement chapter save.

If the game crashes during the fight, handle that through the [PC troubleshooting checklist](/pc-stutter-after-halloween-update/) and the official crash-report path. A crash does not demonstrate that a combat strategy failed. Keep the platform, current build, boss phase and last action available for the report.

Return to the encounter with one practical improvement: a shorter Raphael punish, a recognizable endpoint to the Entity's sequence, or a power used with a fallback. These are specific decisions you can evaluate without unsupported chapter counts, completion-rate calculations or a promise that one weapon makes the endgame easy.
