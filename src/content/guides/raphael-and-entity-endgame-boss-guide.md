Valor Mortis players keep asking the same question about the last stretch of the campaign: is there a difficulty spike after the Orphan? A Steam discussion called "difficulty spike" was still getting replies on November 3, 2026. Its opening post describes the Orphan as a fairly relaxed fight, followed by a jump in difficulty for Captain Raphael and the final boss. Recent English Steam reviews point the same way. One review calls the bosses mostly easy apart from the final boss, which it describes as genuinely difficult. Another says only the last boss made them think.

This guide covers the two endgame fights: **Captain Raphael of the Nephtis Superioris** at the end of Chapter 10, and **the Entity, Voice of the Void**, in the Entropy finale. It also explains how the October patches changed both encounters. Everything here comes from the official Steam patch notes, Steam's global achievement stats, and player reports in the Steam discussions. We do not publish health totals or damage numbers, because the game does not show them and nobody has verified them.

## Where the endgame sits in the campaign

According to our [full walkthrough](/guides/walkthrough/), **Chapter 9: In Ruins** ends with the Orphan. **Chapter 10: Shackled** takes you through the Fortress, which has six Lanterns, and ends with Captain Raphael. **Chapter 11: Reflection**, set in Napoleon's Prison, has two Lanterns and no boss. The **Entropy** finale has no Lanterns of its own and ends with the Entity. Because the finale has nowhere to rest, the Lantern before it is where your preparation has to be finished.

## What the achievement data says about the spike

As of November 4, 2026, Steam's global achievement percentages for the last three boss achievements (all Hidden on Steam) are:

| Achievement | Requirement | Global unlock rate |
|---|---|---|
| Coup de Grâce (Hidden) | Defeat the Orphan | 29.0% |
| Behemoth (Hidden) | Defeat Nepthis Superioris | 26.2% |
| In His Own Image (Hidden) | Defeat The Entity | 24.2% |

Seen from the forum, the endgame looks like a wall. The numbers tell a gentler story. About nine in ten players who beat the Orphan go on to beat Raphael, and a similar share of those go on to beat the Entity. The larger drop on the ladder comes much earlier, at General Lothaire, which we cover in [how to beat General Lothaire](/how-to-beat-general-lothaire/). So the endgame bosses are harder than what came before, but players who reach them usually finish. The practical goal is to arrive prepared enough that the fights take less time and fewer resets.

## Before Chapter 10: finish one of each

The same achievement list shows how far most finishers have pushed their gear. **Bladesmith** (one melee weapon at maximum) is at 31.8%, **Gun of Glory** (one firearm at maximum) at 26.7%, and **Transmutation Artifex** (one Transmutation at maximum) at 25.6%. Our [weapons and upgrades guide](/guides/weapons-and-upgrades/) makes the same recommendation the endgame rewards: one fully upgraded melee weapon, one fully upgraded firearm, and one fully upgraded Transmutation beat three half-built sets.

Check your Ferrofluid Flask upgrades too. The October 21 patch fixed a bug where upgrading your flask might not be saved. If your flask count looks lower than you remember after a reload, that is worth checking before you blame the boss. The difficulty-spike thread's opening poster said they felt underpowered at the end because they had missed several of the collectible upgrades along the way.

## Captain Raphael: an endurance test, not a puzzle

The players in the spike thread disagree on how hard Raphael is, but they describe him in similar terms. One called him more of an endurance test than a spike, with more health but no complicated or hard-to-read patterns. That player beat him in around 20 minutes with the Rapier, using six potions. Another said Raphael "wasn't that bad" with the Claymore. A third said he is not very aggressive but can kill you quickly, and called him a fair fight. One player even needed more attempts for the Orphan than for Raphael or the final boss.

Those reports point to a simple plan:

- **Pace yourself for a long fight.** A long health bar punishes greed more than slow play. Take the safe punish, then reset your spacing.
- **Do not rely on old stagger tricks.** The October 21 patch tweaked Raphael's balance to adjust his ability to block and stagger, and made his strafe, walk, and shoot speeds more consistent. The October 27 update fixed a bug where Raphael could stop attacking if a Rapier heavy attack staggered him at the right moment. A clip showing him standing still is showing a bug that has been fixed.
- **Treat the Fortress as a warning.** One player noted that the Chapter 10 level leading to Raphael had some of the toughest normal and elite enemies so far, including the final elite fight in the Eternal Guard quest. **We Wear the Same Colors**, the achievement for defeating every Eternal Guardist, is at 15.4%. If those fights feel hard, your build probably needs work before the boss door.

The October 21 patch also fixed a sliding bug on Raphael and a progression block when you and he die at the same moment.

## The Entity, Voice of the Void: a review of everything

The finale reuses the campaign. The Entity brings back attacks from earlier bosses. The patch notes even refer to his "recruited bosses", spawned enemies that can use Fire Transmutations. That makes the fight a test of every red, unblockable attack you have learned to dodge.

Player reports focus on the second phase. One described it as manageable until the Entity starts his long combo, which is tiring to keep up with. Another called phase two an "endless combo" and changed playstyle for it, swapping a Rapier parry approach for a heavier weapon with more blocking, and switching to dodging only for those long strings. A third complained about the long gap between phases. Expect that gap, and do not spend resources while you wait.

Practical habits for the final fight:

- **Learn the phase two combo before you try to punish it.** If you cannot see the end of the string, dodge until it ends and only then commit.
- **Respect the recruited bosses.** The October 27 update fixed a bug where Fire Transmutations might not stop being used by spawned enemies in this fight, and fixed the Entity's shield sometimes appearing in the wrong position.
- **Retest Stasis.** The October 21 patch fixed several bugs with how Stasis was applied to the Entity, how it behaved, and how long it lasted. It also fixed him walking and attacking at the same time, vanishing when aimed at during a teleport, and shooting at nothing.

## When the problem is the game, not the fight

One player in the spike thread said most of their final-boss retries came from crashes rather than difficulty. If the game crashes, it is not a skill problem. Update first, then work through our [PC stutter checklist](/pc-stutter-after-halloween-update/). The October 21 announcement says Support can provide replacement save files at each chapter for soft-locked progression. If a simultaneous death leaves you without **Behemoth** or **In His Own Image**, the October 27 update says the missing achievements should unlock when you press Continue on the main menu. The [boss achievement recovery checklist](/boss-achievement-and-chapter-recovery/) walks through that step.

## After the credits

**Nephtis Superioris**, the achievement for unlocking every other achievement, was at 6.1% on November 4, 2026. That leaves plenty of cleanup for finishers. The developers have also announced a Major Story DLC for next year in their launch post.
