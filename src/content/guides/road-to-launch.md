Valor Mortis has had an eventful pre-launch summer. This page tracks the confirmed timeline — the release-date move, the Demo's return, and the events One More Level is taking it to — all from official announcements.

## The release date moved to October 13

One More Level confirmed the full game now launches **October 13, 2026** on Steam. The studio framed the shift honestly: "It's going to be a crazy few months of gaming, but we believe we're creating something unique and distinct with our first-person soulslike." For the confirmed platform details, see the [release date page](/release-date/).

## The Demo came back for the summer

Originally released on **June 7, 2026**, the free Demo was so well received that it was extended, then brought back again for a summer window of **July 20 – August 7**. One More Level's reason was simple: they want more player impressions to keep improving the game before launch. The Demo covers early chapters and — as of the summer return — includes **Chapter 7**, with its corrupted villagers, the Rapier, and the Cannonhand Dubois encounter. See our [Chapter 7 guide](/demo/chapter-7/).

## A Steam Next Fest standout

Steam named Valor Mortis one of the **top Demos of Next Fest**, and to celebrate, the studio extended the Demo by two weeks at the time. That recognition, plus a reported **speedrun of Chapter 7 in under three minutes**, points to a movement-focused design — unsurprising from the team behind **Ghostrunner**, which the developer itself referenced when talking about the platforming and speed.

## The event tour

Through the summer, One More Level is taking Valor Mortis to a run of events, including **COREBLAZER**, **ChinaJoy**, and the Steam **"From Poland with Love"** showcase. The studio's framing: "It's really exciting that we get to meet players around the world with our game" as they approach launch.

## What it signals

Read together, these are the moves of a studio confident enough to keep putting the game in front of people right up to release: a demo it keeps re-opening, a public speedrun scene, and a steady patch cadence (see [performance and upscaling](/demo/performance-and-upscaling/)). None of it changes the confirmed facts — free Demo now, full game **October 13, 2026** on Steam — but it does tell you the runway to launch is being used to polish, not to go quiet.
