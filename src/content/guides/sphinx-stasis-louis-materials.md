After updating Valor Mortis, plan for the Sphinx to resume attacking after a Stasis setup and critical strike. If you have already defeated the boss and Louis stopped offering specific materials, check his stock on the same campaign before replaying anything. The [November 23 developer notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2828710/announcements/) describe a fight fix and a separate merchant fix. This guide uses those two changes to help you finish the encounter and verify the purchase you need.

## Match the symptom to the part of the run

| What you see | First thing to check |
| --- | --- |
| The Sphinx remains motionless after Stasis and a critical attack | Confirm your installed update and record exactly when the boss stopped acting. |
| The Sphinx resumes attacking after the critical strike | Leave time to reposition and read the next action before spending another power. |
| Louis opens his shop, but a material disappeared after the kill | Check the same campaign and record the exact item you had previously seen for sale. |
| The shop will not open, or the boss defeat itself appears to have been lost | Check the dialogue and campaign state separately from the material listing. |

These checks apply to the released game. The November 23 developer post is the reference for the two fixes. Confirm the update offered by your own launcher or console before using a clip or forum post from a different build. Keep the fight and shop results in separate notes so you can report which one actually persists.

## Plan an exit after your critical strike

The boss was previously able to stop acting for the rest of the encounter if the player used Stasis just before performing a critical attack. If an older clip shows someone standing safely next to an inactive Sphinx, treat it as footage of that interaction. A usable plan for the updated fight starts with the next attack the boss might make after your strike.

1. **Enter with a repeatable opening.** Pick one approach you already recognize. Keep the same weapon and technique for your first few attempts. Start by reading the boss’s normal movement and identifying the space you want to move toward once a critical action ends.
2. **Check the opportunity before committing.** Watch the encounter’s actual cue for a critical. If you are using Stasis, observe the boss state and your available power before spending it. A visible opening followed by a clean exit is a useful comparison point on the next run.
3. **Leave time for the response.** Once the critical resolves, regain a clear view of the boss and see what it does. If it resumes pressure while you are still nearby, make your first decision about spacing and defense. Save the next offensive commitment for an opening you can read.
4. **Change one thing after a failed attempt.** If the damage went through and you were hit immediately afterward, change where you recover before replacing the whole loadout. If the attack never started, check the activation cue first. If the boss still freezes indefinitely on the updated build, capture that behavior separately.

Keep a fallback that works before you have a critical available. In an attempt where the opening does not appear, use a familiar defensive response to create space, then reassess. This lets you continue testing without using every resource on a single sequence. For more on reading the end of your attack and finding space, use the [Rapier and Saber practice guide](/rapier-saber-critical-combat-practice/).

## Check Louis’s shop after the defeat

The developer notes report that some materials could disappear from Louis’s inventory after the Sphinx kill. If your current campaign is still before the encounter, take a quick inventory of the one or two materials you actually need when you can visit Louis normally. Save the exact names or a screenshot with the shop open. You will have a useful baseline when you return. If your campaign is already past the fight, start with the material the upgrade screen currently asks for.

1. **Confirm you loaded the completed campaign.** Check the last objective or checkpoint you recognize before travelling back to Louis. If the encounter’s completion did not persist, move to the [chapter recovery guide](/boss-achievement-and-chapter-recovery/) and diagnose that state first.
2. **Open the shop and inspect the matching listing.** Compare the full item name, the pane you are viewing, and the stock indication where one appears. A listed item that you cannot currently buy because of your balance tells you more than a generic note that the upgrade is blocked.
3. **Record the result before changing state.** Capture the item or the section where it previously appeared. If a normal reload is available, reopen the same campaign once and compare Louis’s stock again. Leave any earlier save intact while you establish whether the result repeats.
4. **Choose the next step from the result.** If the material is available, buy what the upgrade actually asks for and verify the new inventory count. If a previously available material stays missing, preserve the comparison for support and keep unspent currency for when you can confirm an alternative.

The patch describes the affected stock broadly. Use the live shop for exact item names and purchase availability. Current vial and grenade prices belong to the separate [consumable prices guide](/valor-mortis-consumable-prices-vials-grenades/). If a different material is on sale, compare it against the upgrade requirement before buying it.

## Make a useful report if the issue repeats

For the fight, record the platform, version where shown, whether Stasis was active, whether the critical struck, and what the Sphinx did next. For the shop, record the material’s exact name, when you last saw it, whether the Sphinx defeat persisted, and what Louis displayed after a normal reload. Include a before-and-after comparison if you already have one. These details give support a specific state to investigate and let you continue from a known checkpoint when it is safe to do so.
