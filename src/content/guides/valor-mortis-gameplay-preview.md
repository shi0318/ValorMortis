## What Valor Mortis is built around

The official store description presents a first-person action soulslike set in a Napoleon-era world distorted by dark magic. The player is a fallen soldier of Napoleon's Grande Armée, resurrected into a twisted Paris. The listed gameplay pillars include melee, firearms, parkour-style mobility, and resurrection.

Those pillars make a practical Demo test possible: learn the parry rhythm, compare close-range and firearm options, understand how movement changes an encounter, and observe what resurrection does in the tested build. The description does not publish a complete damage formula, final boss list, full map, or retail progression table.

## What the separate Demo can prove

The free Demo is a separate Steam product with App ID **4641840**. Its store listing includes **Save Anytime**, and the Demo was released on **June 7, 2026**. It is not the same Steam product as the full game, which uses App ID **2828710** and lists October 13, 2026.

The Demo can provide reproducible observations about its own build: controller behavior, movement feel, accessibility options, early encounters, performance, and the weapons or powers visible in its slice. It cannot prove that every enemy position, checkpoint, ability cost, or PC requirement will be identical in the retail game.

## A clean combat test route

When testing the Demo, change one variable at a time:

1. Record the Demo version, resolution, input device, and graphics preset.
2. Test a parry against the same attack pattern and note the visible result.
3. Repeat with a dodge or mobility option so the comparison is not based on one missed input.
4. Test a firearm and melee weapon in separate encounters.
5. Record what resurrection changes and whether the encounter state persists after returning.

This does not create a universal build recommendation. It gives the eventual retail guide a baseline that can be checked again on October 13.

## Demo progress and the full release

No official source tracked here promises that Demo saves, inventory, upgrades, or story progress transfer to the full game. The safe answer is **not confirmed**. Keep Demo knowledge as preparation, but do not purchase or plan a retail route around an assumed save transfer.

The Demo availability window can also change independently from the full-game date. If the Demo page is not currently offering an installer, use the walkthrough as pre-release documentation and rely on the official Steam product page for access status.

## PC requirements are product-specific

Steam lists a separate Demo specification, including Windows 11, 16 GB RAM, DirectX 12, an SSD, and the listed CPU/GPU class. The Demo page itself says its specification can change for the final release. Do not describe meeting the Demo requirement as proof that a PC meets the retail requirement; compare the two live Steam pages separately.

## What to verify on October 13

At full release, record the retail build before turning Demo observations into permanent guide claims. Re-test resurrection, parry timing, target lock, mobility, Rapier, Musket, Shield Transmutation, and Nephtoglobin-related powers against the shipped game. Keep any changed Demo behavior visible as a versioned note rather than silently rewriting history.
