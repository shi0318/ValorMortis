The arsenal in **Valor Mortis** is not intended to be a museum list of Napoleonic weapons. One More Level says its weapons blend historical-era themes with supernatural fiction and technology enabled by the discovery of **Nephtoglobin**. The official Demo updates name a **Rapier**, **Musket**, and **Shield Transmutation**, while the main premise puts William against Napoleon's plague-ridden forces and the named **Eternal Guard**.

Use this page to understand the confirmed categories and prepare for fights. Weapon stats, ammunition, scaling, and the complete retail arsenal remain unpublished.

## Read the Eternal Guard as a mobility problem

The official enemy material names Napoleon's **Eternal Guard** as a plague-ridden faction, and identifies **The Relentless** as elite loyalists with greatswords and concealed faces. The developer's combat update also says mobility is the intended counter to the Guard, alongside improvements to target lock, camera tracking, target prioritisation, and ranged/melee range.

That gives a practical encounter order:

1. Keep an exit route open before committing to a melee exchange.
2. Separate one Guard from a group with terrain or movement.
3. Use a parry or dash to create the opening; do not stand still trading into a greatsword.
4. Test the Musket or another ranged option after the target is isolated.
5. Finish only after the actual enemy state is visible and confirmed.

This is an interpretation of the official mobility guidance, not a promise that every Guard has the same weakness.

## Do not confuse transmutation with a weapon slot

The official language uses **transmutation** for supernatural powers, including the named Shield Transmutation. It has not published a final menu layout or whether every transmutation shares a resource. Keep a separate note for weapon input, power input, and movement input until the retail interface is available.

## Demo preparation

The Demo's Lighthouse section is the best official place to practise the new named tools. Use it to capture:

- Rapier attack and parry animations;
- Musket aim and reload behaviour;
- Shield Transmutation activation and duration;
- how the camera behaves in multi-enemy fights;
- which encounters reward movement rather than stationary blocking.

Mark every observation as **Demo-tested**. It is not automatically a launch guarantee.
